Hi mga ka-Humid!
Rain in the afternoon, mosquitoes by evening. Classic tag-ulan.
The good news is that a lot of mosquito breeding spots are right inside our homes, so we can actually do something about them.

This checklist follows the US CDC's mosquito prevention guide.

![Mosquito check card](/assets/cards/ph-mosquito-free-home-tag-ulan-summary.png)

## Why weekly?

CDC's advice is to check for water-holding containers indoors and outdoors.
**Once a week**, empty and scrub, turn over, cover, or throw out anything that holds water.

The scrubbing part matters. Just pouring the water out isn't the whole job.

## 5 spots to check

1. **Plant saucers** under your pots, inside and outside
2. **Timba and tabo** in the CR or laundry area
3. **Kids' toys** left in the yard or on the balcony
4. **Pet water bowls**: change the water and scrub the bowl
5. **Trash bins** whose lids collect rainwater

Old tires and unused containers in the garage count, too.
CDC lists tires, buckets, toys, pools and flowerpot saucers as the usual suspects.

## Keep them out

CDC suggests screens on windows and doors and patching any holes.
If you have aircon, using it helps as well.

## Going out? Repellent basics

Look for repellents with active ingredients like DEET, picaridin, IR3535, oil of lemon eucalyptus (OLE) or PMD, and follow the label.
Using sunscreen too? CDC says sunscreen goes on first, repellent second.

For kids under **3 years old**, skip products with OLE or PMD.

## Small buys that help

A lid for the water drum, a small brush for pet bowls, and window screens sized to your frames.
Check that screens can be taken out for washing.

Stay dry and bite-free, sis!
