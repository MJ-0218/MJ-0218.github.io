Hi mga ka-Humid!
If your closet smells a bit musty lately, you're not alone.
Wet months and closed windows are basically a party invitation for amag.

The good news: you don't need fancy tricks.
The US EPA's mould guide boils it down to one idea, and it's a simple one.

![Mould prevention checklist card](/assets/cards/ph-rainy-season-mould-home-checklist-summary.png)

## The one rule: control the moisture

EPA puts it plainly: **the key to mould control is moisture control.**
Mould needs damp spots to grow, so the less moisture you leave around, the fewer chances it gets.

That's why wiping one wet wall won't fix things if the leak above it keeps dripping.
Find where the water comes from first.

## Know your number: indoor humidity

EPA suggests keeping indoor humidity **below 60 percent**, and ideally **between 30 and 50 percent**.
A small humidity meter (hygrometer) lets you check this at home.

In our weather, reaching the low end of that range is tough, and that's okay.
The goal is to stay out of the very damp zone, especially in bedrooms and closets.

## Dry things fast

When something gets wet from a leak or a flood, EPA says to dry it **within 24–48 hours** to prevent mould.
So don't leave that wet doormat or soaked box sitting in a corner "for later".

Quick habits that help:

- Run the bathroom fan or open the window when you shower.
- Use the exhaust fan or open a window while cooking.
- Turn on the aircon or a dehumidifier on very humid days.
- Watch for water droplets on windows, walls or pipes. EPA says condensation can be a sign of high humidity, so dry it and find the source.

## Already have amag? Size it up

EPA says that if the mouldy area is less than about **10 square feet** (roughly a 3 ft by 3 ft patch), most people can handle it themselves.
For hard surfaces, scrub with detergent and water, then dry completely.

Some things can't really be saved.
Porous stuff like ceiling tiles and carpet may need to be thrown away once mould gets in.

If the patch is bigger than that, or there was a lot of water damage, it's time to call a professional.

## Shopping for a dehumidifier?

Look at the room size it's made for, how big the water tank is, and whether it can drain continuously.
A unit with a built-in humidity display saves you from buying a separate meter.

Stay dry, sis, and may your closets smell fresh all season!
