Hi mga ka-Humid!
Quick question: how old is the mascara in your kikay kit right now?
If you have to think about it, this post is for you.

Eye makeup is usually safe, but FDA explains that using it the wrong way can let bacteria or fungi grow in it.
Here are the habits that matter most, especially in our heat.

![Mascara age card](/assets/cards/ph-mascara-eye-makeup-hot-weather-summary.png)

## The 2-to-4-month rule

FDA notes that manufacturers usually recommend **discarding mascara 2 to 4 months after purchase**.
Write the date you opened it on the tube with a marker. Future you will thank you.

And if it has dried up, let it go.
FDA warns against adding saliva or water to revive it, because that can bring in bacteria and dilute the preservative.

## Heat is not your makeup's friend

FDA says **not to store cosmetics above 85°F**, which is about 29°C.
Long stretches in a hot car, for example, can make the preservative break down faster.

That's a normal afternoon here, so:

- Keep your makeup pouch out of the car dashboard and away from sunny windowsills.
- Store it in a drawer or cabinet, not right next to the bathroom shower.

## Don't share, don't swap

Not even with your bestie, sis.
FDA says another person's germs may be hazardous to you.

Store testers are riskier still, since many people use the same sample.
If you want to try a shade, ask for a clean, single-use applicator like a cotton swab.

## Other quick rules

- Stop using any eye product that irritates your eyes. If it doesn't settle, see a doctor.
- Had an eye infection? Throw out the eye makeup you were using then.
- Wash your hands before applying.
- Never apply or remove eye makeup in a moving jeep, bus or car. One bump and the wand can scratch your eye.
- Use products made for the eye area. Lip liner is not eyeliner.

## Shopping for a new mascara?

Since you'll replace it every few months, a smaller tube can make more sense than a big one.
Check the ingredient list on the box, too. FDA notes that retail cosmetics must list their ingredients.

Stay pretty and stay safe!
