Hi, mga ka-beauty!
Rebond, keratin, Brazilian blowout... straight hair before the holidays is a whole mood.
But before you sit in that salon chair, here's what the US FDA wants you to know.

![Hair smoothing check card](/assets/cards/ph-hair-rebond-formaldehyde-salon-questions-summary.png)

## What happens during smoothing
The stylist puts a smoothing solution on your hair, then uses heat to seal it in.
FDA says these products **often contain formaldehyde**.

When the solution is heated, formaldehyde is **released into the air as a gas**.
In a salon without good ventilation, both you and the stylist can breathe it in.

## The 3 words to look for
On the ingredient list, check for:

- **Formaldehyde**
- **Formalin**
- **Methylene glycol**

They're the names FDA flags for formaldehyde-related ingredients.

## 3 questions to ask your salon
1. Does this product contain formaldehyde, formalin or methylene glycol?
2. Can I see the box or the Safety Data Sheet?
3. Is the room well ventilated while you flat-iron?

FDA says if your stylist isn't sure, you may not want to use the product, especially if you've reacted before.

## Why it matters
According to the US National Cancer Institute, cited by FDA, formaldehyde in the air above 0.1 ppm can cause watery eyes, burning in the nose and throat, and coughing in some people.

Some people are very sensitive; others don't react at all.

## Buying a DIY kit online?
FDA says retail products must list their ingredients. If a kit has no list, **don't buy it**.

If you get a bad reaction after a treatment, tell your doctor.
Ingat, and enjoy that silky hair!
