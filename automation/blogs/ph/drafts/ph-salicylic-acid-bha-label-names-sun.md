"BHA" is everywhere on toners and cleansers lately.
Here's how to spot it on a label, plus the precautions the US FDA lists.

![BHA label card](/assets/cards/ph-salicylic-acid-bha-label-names-sun-summary.png)

## Names to look for
According to the US FDA, BHA ingredients may be listed as:
- **salicylic acid** (or related names like salicylate, sodium salicylate, willow extract)
- beta hydroxybutanoic acid
- tropic acid
- trethocanic acid

The BHA used most often in cosmetics is **salicylic acid**.

## The FDA's precautions
- **Patch test** a small area first.
- If you get irritation or **stinging that won't go away**, stop and see a doctor.
- **Follow the label** and don't use it more often than it says.
- **Avoid** using BHA products on babies and kids.
- **Use sun protection** while you're using a BHA product.

## Why sun protection matters
An industry safety panel said salicylic acid products should either contain sunscreen or come with directions to use other sun protection.
Under our sun, that's a big one.

## Quick shopping tip
Check the **ingredient list and directions** before checking out, especially if you're stacking several exfoliating products.
