## What talc is
Talc is a **natural mineral mined from the earth**.
The US FDA says it's used in many cosmetics, "from baby powder to blush."

## Why brands use it
According to the FDA, talc can help a product:
- **absorb moisture**
- **prevent caking**
- make face makeup **opaque**
- **feel smoother** on skin

That's why it's so common in powders made for humid days.

## The concern you may have heard about
Talc and asbestos are both natural minerals and can be found close together in the ground.
The FDA notes there's a **potential for contamination**, so mining sites should be chosen carefully and the ore tested properly.

The FDA has kept testing talc-containing cosmetics for asbestos.

## How to shop for powder
- Look at the **ingredient list**: if talc is in it, you'll see "talc."
- If you prefer to skip it, look for **"talc-free"** and double-check the list.
- Pick sellers who show the **full ingredient list** clearly.
