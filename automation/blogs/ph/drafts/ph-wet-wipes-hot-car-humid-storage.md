Wet wipes live in our bags, cars and diaper bags.
But in our heat, where you keep them matters. Here's what the US FDA says.

![Wet wipes card](/assets/cards/ph-wet-wipes-hot-car-humid-storage-summary.png)

## Why heat is a problem
FDA says wipes exposed to extreme temperatures, like a **hot car**, can **dry out**.
Heat can also change the ingredients.

For example, the **preservatives** that protect against bacteria and mold may **break down** and stop doing their job.
Bacteria and mold can also **grow faster in a warm place**.

## Storage habits that help
- Keep the pack **tightly closed** so wipes don't dry out.
- Don't leave them sitting in a parked car under the sun.
- Throw used wipes away right away to avoid cross contamination.

## Using them safely
- Use wipes only on **unbroken, intact skin**.
- Use them only for what they're made for, and follow the label.
- Let your skin **dry thoroughly** afterward.

## When to stop
If you get a rash, redness, irritation or burning where the wipe touched your skin, stop using it and see a doctor.
FDA also wants to hear about it, even if all you notice is an **off color or a bad smell**.
