Halloween parties mean false lashes, face paint and glitter everywhere.
If latex bothers your skin, here's how to check before you buy.

![Latex label card](/assets/cards/ph-eyelash-glue-face-paint-latex-label-summary.png)

## Where latex usually hides
The US FDA lists **body paints, theatrical cosmetics, hair bonding adhesives and eyelash adhesives** as products that commonly contain natural rubber latex.

## No warning, but there's an ingredient list
Cosmetics **don't have to carry a latex allergy warning**.
They do have to list ingredients, so the list is your best tool.

## Names that mean latex
- Natural Rubber Latex
- Natural Latex
- Rubber Latex
- Latex
- Natural Centrifuged Latex
- Natural Liquid Latex
- Aqueous Latex
- Adhesive Latex

## Re-check every time
Makers can **change ingredients at any time**.
So even your usual lash glue deserves a quick look each time you reorder.

## If you react
Stop using it and contact your **healthcare professional**.
