## The 2–4 month rule
According to the US FDA, manufacturers usually recommend throwing out mascara **two to four months after purchase**.
That's much shorter than most of us actually keep it, di ba?

## Why mascara goes off so fast
Every time you use it, the wand picks up bacteria and fungi, then goes right back into the tube.
FDA says applicators, especially mascara wands, get exposed each time you use them.

Eye-area products tend to have shorter shelf lives than other makeup.
And when they go bad, they may cause eye infections that can be serious.

## Our humid weather doesn't help
FDA notes that moisture, like in a bathroom, can make it easier for bacteria and fungi to grow.
Heat matters too: it can make preservatives break down faster.

So skip storing your makeup bag in the CR or inside a parked car under the sun.

## When it dries up
Tempting to add a few drops of water to revive it, ha?
FDA says don't: if mascara becomes dry, **throw it away**.

Adding water, or worse, saliva, puts bacteria straight into the tube.

## Quick habits that help
- Write the opening date on the tube with a marker.
- Don't share mascara, even with your bestie. You might be sharing an infection.
- If you get an eye infection, see your doctor, stop using all eye makeup, and toss the ones you were using.

Small habit, big difference for your eyes.
