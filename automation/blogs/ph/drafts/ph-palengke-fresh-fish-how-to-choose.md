Sunday palengke run for isda and hipon?
Here's how the US FDA says to tell if seafood is fresh before you pay.

![Fresh fish card](/assets/cards/ph-palengke-fresh-fish-how-to-choose-summary.png)

## Start with where it's kept
FDA says to buy fish that's **refrigerated or on a thick bed of fresh ice**.
And don't judge by color alone, since color can change for many reasons and isn't a sign of freshness by itself.

## Whole fish: 4 quick checks
- **Smell:** fresh and mild, never fishy, sour or ammonia-like.
- **Eyes:** clear and shiny.
- **Gills:** red, with no odor.
- **Flesh:** firm, and it should spring back when you press it.

## Hipon and other shellfish
Shrimp flesh should look **clear with a pearl-like color** and have little or no smell.

For tahong, talaba and halaan:
- Skip any with **cracked or broken shells**.
- Do the **tap test**. Live ones close when you tap the shell. If they don't close, don't pick them.

Live crabs should still **move their legs**.

## Getting it home
If you'll cook it within **2 days**, keep it in a clean fridge at **4°C (40°F) or colder**.
If not, wrap it tightly and freeze it.

And if raw or cooked seafood smells sour, rancid or fishy, FDA says don't eat it.
