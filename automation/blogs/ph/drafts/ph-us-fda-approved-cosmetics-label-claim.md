Hi, mga ka-Humid!
Seen an imported cream or lotion listing that says "US FDA approved"?
Here's what the US FDA itself says about that.

![US FDA approved card](/assets/cards/ph-us-fda-approved-cosmetics-label-claim-summary.png)

## The US FDA doesn't approve cosmetics
In its own words: "The FDA doesn't approve cosmetics."
Cosmetics must be safe for their intended use and properly labeled, but they don't need US FDA approval before they're sold.

That covers things like perfume, makeup, moisturizers, shampoo, hair dye and face or body cleansers.

## The one exception
**Color additives** in cosmetics (other than coal-tar hair dyes) do need US FDA approval for their use.

## About that FDA logo
FDA says its logo is for **official government use only**.
Misusing it may violate US federal law, and it shouldn't be used to suggest the agency endorses a product.

## What to do instead
- Read the **full ingredient list** and directions.
- Be wary of "approved" badges or FDA logos on a cosmetic listing.

One more thing: this is about the **US** FDA. The Philippines, for example, has their own agency with a similar name and their own rules, so check your local regulator's system too.
