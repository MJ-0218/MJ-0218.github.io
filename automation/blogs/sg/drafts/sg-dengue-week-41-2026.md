Hi everyone! Quick weather-and-wellbeing check for this week.
NEA reported **60 dengue cases** in the week ending 17 October 2026, which is 19 fewer than the week before.
Good news, but not a reason to relax just yet.

![Dengue in Singapore this week summary](/assets/cards/sg-dengue-week-41-2026-summary.png)

## Where the red clusters are
As of 19 October 2026, there were eight active dengue clusters.
Three of them were on red alert, which NEA uses for high-risk areas with 10 or more cases.

![Red dengue clusters table](/assets/cards/sg-dengue-week-41-2026-clusters.png)

If you live or work near one of these, take the home check below seriously this week.
Cluster lists change often, so look up NEA's cluster page before you go by an old screenshot.

## Why it still matters in late October

Singapore's traditional peak dengue season runs from May to October.
NEA also points out that our tropical climate and dense living let mosquitoes thrive all year round.

So even as the peak season winds down, a single forgotten flowerpot plate can keep the cycle going.
It's a small thing.
That's exactly why it's easy to miss.

## The B-L-O-C-K check, step by step
NEA's Mozzie Wipeout steps take about as long as making a cup of kopi:

- **B**reak up hardened soil in your plant pots.
- **L**ift and empty flowerpot plates.
- **O**verturn pails and wipe their rims.
- **C**hange the water in vases.
- **K**eep roof gutters clear and place BTI insecticide inside.

Make it a weekly habit, maybe every Sunday before you water the plants.

## If you're in a cluster area: S-A-W
For residents in cluster areas, or anyone diagnosed with or suspected to have dengue, NEA recommends three more actions to prevent bites:

- **S**pray insecticide in dark corners around the house.
- **A**pply insect repellent regularly.
- **W**ear long-sleeve tops and long pants.

Feeling unwell with symptoms that might be dengue? See a doctor early. Early diagnosis helps with better care.

## What about Zika and chikungunya?
Some reassuring bits from the same NEA update.
There are currently no Zika clusters or areas with likely Zika transmission in Singapore.

There are also no chikungunya clusters right now.
NEA says most chikungunya infections were in people who had recently travelled to affected areas.
Back from a trip and feeling feverish? Mention your travel when you see the doctor.

## Quick humid-home extras
- Check under the fridge tray and the dish rack. Water sits there quietly.
- Keep bathroom pails upside down when you're not using them.
- Let NEA officers inspect if they knock. It helps stop transmission faster.

Stay dry, stay bite-free, and have a good week ahead!
