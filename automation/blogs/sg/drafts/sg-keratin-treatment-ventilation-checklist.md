Hello Singapore!
Humidity and frizz go hand in hand here, so it's no surprise keratin and smoothing treatments are popular.
Here's a short, practical checklist based on the US FDA's guidance.

![Smoothing treatment card](/assets/cards/sg-keratin-treatment-ventilation-checklist-summary.png)

## Why the air in the room matters
Many smoothing solutions contain formaldehyde, FDA says.
The heat step releases it **into the air as a gas**, so poor ventilation puts both client and stylist at risk.

FDA also notes that **the longer and stronger the exposure, the higher the potential risk**.
Small, closed rooms can trap fumes, so ask about ventilation before the flat iron comes out.

## Check the label or the safety sheet
Look for **formaldehyde, formalin or methylene glycol** in the ingredients.

Professional-only products may not carry a full ingredient list.
Instead, they should have a **Safety Data Sheet (SDS)** that lists hazards, which you can ask to see.

## Signs to stop and step out
Watery eyes, a burning nose or throat, coughing or nausea are all reactions FDA lists.
If you feel any of these mid-treatment, tell your stylist and get fresh air.

Skin sensitization can also build up with repeated contact, so a reaction can show up even after past treatments went fine.

## Your quick checklist
- Ask: "Does this contain formaldehyde, formalin or methylene glycol?"
- Ask to see the box or SDS.
- Choose a well-ventilated spot in the salon.
- Report any bad reaction to your doctor.

Here's to smooth hair and easy breathing!
