Hello Singapore!
Packing for a year-end trip and trying to save space?
If you wear contacts, one common hack is a no-go, according to the US FDA.

![Lens solution card](/assets/cards/sg-contact-lens-solution-travel-bottle-summary.png)

## Don't decant your solution
FDA says not to transfer contact lens solution into **smaller travel-size containers**.
It can affect the **sterility** of the solution and lead to an eye infection.

You could also end up using a liquid that isn't meant for your eyes by mistake.

## 5 more rules worth knowing
1. **Check the date.** Don't use solution past its expiration or discard date.
2. **Saline is for rinsing only.** It doesn't clean or disinfect lenses.
3. **Don't top off.** Throw out leftover solution in the case after each use.
4. **No water at all**, including tap or bottled water.
5. **Replace your case** every 3 months, or as your eye doctor advises.

## Why it matters
FDA notes that incorrect solution care can raise the risk of **eye infections and corneal ulcers**, which can develop quickly.

Safe travels!
