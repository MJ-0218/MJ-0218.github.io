Hello Singapore!
Supermarket shelves have plenty of "fruit and vegetable wash" bottles.
Before you add one to your trolley, here's what the US FDA says.

![Produce wash card](/assets/cards/sg-fruit-vegetable-wash-do-you-need-it-summary.png)

## The short answer
FDA does **not** recommend washing fruits and vegetables with soap, detergent or commercial produce wash.
Its consumer tips say there's **no need to use soap or a produce wash**.

## Why not?
Two reasons from FDA:

- **Produce is porous.** Soap and household detergents can be absorbed, even after thorough rinsing, and can make you sick.
- **Unknowns.** The safety of produce-wash residues isn't known, and their effectiveness hasn't been tested.

## What to do instead
- Gently rub produce under **plain running water**.
- Scrub firm produce like **melons and cucumbers** with a clean brush.
- Dry with a **clean cloth or paper towel**.
- For lettuce or cabbage, remove the **outermost leaves**.

## Bagged salad and pre-cut fruit
If the pack says **pre-washed** or **ready-to-eat**, FDA says you can use it without washing again.
Keep pre-cut or packaged produce in the fridge.

## One honest note
Washing **reduces** bacteria but doesn't eliminate it.
So good hand-washing and clean chopping boards still matter.

Save your money for the good fruit instead. Happy shopping!
