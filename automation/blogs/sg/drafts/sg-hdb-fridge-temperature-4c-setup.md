Hello Singapore!
Most of us have a compact fridge that's always a little too full.
Here's how to set it up properly, based on the US FDA's food storage tips.

![Fridge setup card](/assets/cards/sg-hdb-fridge-temperature-4c-setup-summary.png)

## The two numbers to remember
- **Fridge**: at or below **4°C**
- **Freezer**: **-18°C**

Many fridge dials only show numbers, not degrees, so you can't tell the real temperature from them.
That's where a thermometer helps.

## Get a cheap thermometer (or two)
FDA says appliance thermometers are **the best way to know these temperatures**, and they're generally inexpensive.
Put one in the fridge and one in the freezer, **at the front where you can read it easily**.

Then check them **at least once a week**.

## Don't over-pack
FDA's advice is to not crowd the fridge or freezer so tightly that **air can't circulate**.
Cold air needs space to move, especially after a big weekend grocery run.

## Eggs belong inside, not on the door
Keep eggs **in their carton on a shelf**, not in the door rack.
The door is the warmest part of the fridge.

## Cover everything
Store food in **covered containers or sealed bags**, and check leftovers daily.
Wipe up spills straight away, and give the fridge a regular clean.

Five minutes of setup, and your groceries last better. Happy organising!
