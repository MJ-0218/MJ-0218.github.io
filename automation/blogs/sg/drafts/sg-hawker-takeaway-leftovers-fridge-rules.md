Hello Singapore!
Over-ordered at the hawker centre again? Same.
Before that extra char kway teow sits on the counter all evening, here are the rules from the US CDC.

![Tabao leftovers card](/assets/cards/sg-hawker-takeaway-leftovers-fridge-rules-summary.png)

## How long is too long
CDC's line is clear: perishable food shouldn't be left out **more than 2 hours**.
If it's hotter than **90°F (about 32°C)**, make that **1 hour**.

Takeaway that rode home in a warm car or sat at a void deck picnic counts as "hot".

## The numbers on one card
![Leftover cheat sheet](/assets/cards/sg-hawker-takeaway-leftovers-fridge-rules-rules.png)

## Cool it the right way
A big, deep box of rice stays warm in the middle for a long time.
CDC suggests splitting warm food into **several clean, shallow containers** before it goes in the fridge.

It's fine to put small portions in while they're still warm.

## Check your fridge
CDC recommends a fridge at **40°F (about 4°C) or below** and a freezer at **0°F (about -18°C) or below**.
A cheap fridge thermometer tells you if yours is actually there.

## Reheat properly
Heat leftovers to **165°F (about 74°C)** before eating.
If you're not sure, a food thermometer is the only way to know, CDC says.

Enjoy round two of dinner, safely!
