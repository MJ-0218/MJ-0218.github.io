Hello Singapore!
Managing a food allergy at home takes a few steady habits.
Here's a simple checklist based on US FDA guidance.

![Food allergy checklist card](/assets/cards/sg-food-allergy-label-checklist-family-summary.png)

## FDA's 4 everyday tips
1. **Always read food labels.**
2. **Avoid** the foods you're allergic to.
3. Learn the **early symptoms** of a reaction, in case of accidental eating.
4. **Know what to do** if a reaction happens.

## Know the signs
FDA lists symptoms like hives, a rash, an itchy mouth, swelling of the face, tongue or lips, vomiting, coughing or wheezing, and dizziness.
They can show up **within a few minutes to a few hours**.

FDA also says a **mild reaction doesn't always mean the allergy is mild**. Any reaction can lead to anaphylaxis.

## If symptoms start
FDA's steps for someone with a known allergy:
- **Stop eating** the food right away.
- Check whether **emergency treatment** is needed.
- **Get medical help.**

## On imported US foods
US labels must name 9 major allergens, either in brackets after the ingredient or in a "Contains" line.

Careful avoidance matters most. Your doctor is the best person to plan with.

Take care!
