Hello Singapore!
Lash extensions, costume makeup, hair bonding glue: latex shows up in more places than you'd think.
Here's a checklist built from the US FDA's guidance.

![Lash adhesive latex card](/assets/cards/sg-lash-adhesive-latex-free-checklist-summary.png)

## How common is latex allergy?
The FDA says **roughly 1-6%** of the general population is allergic to natural rubber latex.
Sensitivity can **develop over time**, especially with frequent exposure.

## The checklist
- [ ] Scan the ingredients for **"latex"** in any form (natural rubber latex, liquid latex, adhesive latex and so on).
- [ ] Buying online? The list may be on the **package or the seller's website**.
- [ ] Recheck **every purchase**, since formulas can change.
- [ ] At a lash or hair salon, **tell the professional** if you have or suspect a latex allergy.

## A note on pro-only products
Products marketed **only to professionals may not have an ingredient list**.
Salon staff may need to ask the manufacturer.

## If something goes wrong
Stop using the product and see your **healthcare professional**.

Happy shopping!
