Hello Singapore!
Salicylic acid toners and serums have become a staple for many of us.
Before you add one to cart, here's the US FDA's take in checklist form.

![BHA sun checklist card](/assets/cards/sg-bha-toner-sun-sensitivity-checklist-summary.png)

## First, is it BHA?
The most common BHA in cosmetics is **salicylic acid**.
You might also see salicylate, sodium salicylate or willow extract on the list.

## The checklist
- [ ] Try it on a **small patch** of skin first.
- [ ] Read the directions and **stick to the recommended use**.
- [ ] Irritation or prolonged stinging? **Stop** and talk to a doctor.
- [ ] Keep BHA products **away from infants and children**.
- [ ] Use **sun protection** while using it.

## The sunscreen point, explained
An industry review panel concluded salicylic acid is safe as used when formulated to avoid irritation and increased sun sensitivity.
It added that when sun sensitivity is expected, directions should include **daily sun protection**.

## Shopping tip
Compare the directions on different products, and look for clear ingredient lists on the listing.

Happy shopping!
