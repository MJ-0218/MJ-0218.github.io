Hi everyone!
Halloween night outfits are getting serious, and costume lenses are part of the look for many of us.
Your eyes deserve a little planning too, so here's a checklist based on the US FDA's guidance.

![Lens checklist card](/assets/cards/sg-halloween-costume-lenses-checklist-summary.png)

## Treat them like real lenses

The FDA notes that decorative lenses are medical devices, the same as vision-correcting ones.
They're not a one-size-fits-all accessory.

Regulations differ between countries, so check with an optometrist here about what's right for you.

## Before the party

- **Get your eyes checked.** FDA says a licensed eye doctor makes sure lenses fit, and a wrong fit can hurt your eyes.
- **Read the care instructions** and follow them for wearing, cleaning and disinfecting.
- **Buy from a reputable source.** FDA warns that lenses from street stalls, novelty shops or unknown online sellers may be contaminated or counterfeit.

## At the party

- **No sharing.** Your friend's lenses weren't fitted for your eyes.
- If your eyes start to sting or feel uncomfortable, take the lenses out.

## After the party

Take them out and store or discard them exactly as the instructions say.

## Warning signs

FDA lists redness, eye pain that doesn't go away and decreased vision as possible signs of infection.
If you notice any of these, remove the lenses and see an eye doctor right away.

Have a spooky and safe Halloween!
