Hello Singapore!
Year-end sales mean more time at beauty counters in Orchard and the malls.
Before you swipe that tester lipstick on your lips, here's a small reality check.

![Tester hygiene card](/assets/cards/sg-makeup-testers-hygiene-tips-summary.png)

## Testers are the riskiest version
The US FDA says testers at cosmetic counters are **even more likely to become contaminated** than the same products in your home.
Makes sense, right? Many people dip fingers in, and many wands go back into the same tube.

FDA also explains that fingers add microorganisms like bacteria, mould and yeast to a product.

## The FDA's simple fix
If you really want to test before buying, apply it with **a new, unused applicator**, such as a fresh cotton swab.
Most counters keep disposable swabs and spatulas, so just ask.

## How I'd test each type
- **Lipstick:** scrape a little onto a fresh spatula, then swatch on the back of your hand.
- **Foundation:** pump or scoop onto a clean spatula, try it along your jawline with a new sponge.
- **Mascara:** skip the tester wand on your lashes. Use a disposable wand, or just look at the brush.
- **Eyeshadow:** swatch on your hand with a fresh applicator rather than the shared brush.

Eye-area products deserve extra care. FDA notes they can cause eye infections that can be serious.

## Same rule at home
- Don't share makeup with friends; you may be sharing an infection.
- Keep containers and applicators clean.
- Avoid leaving products in a hot car. Heat can break down preservatives.

Swatch smart, and enjoy the sales!
