Hello Singapore!
Shopping for baby wipes or makeup wipes?
The front of the pack says a lot, but the label tells you more. Here's a quick guide based on the US FDA.

![Baby wipes label card](/assets/cards/sg-baby-wipes-label-unscented-fragrance-free-summary.png)

## 'Unscented' isn't the same as 'fragrance free'
FDA says "unscented" usually means a product has **no noticeable scent**.
But it may still contain **masking fragrance** ingredients to hide other smells.

"Fragrance free" products, on the other hand, **shouldn't contain any added fragrance**.
They might still smell a little because of other ingredients.

## What's inside a wipe
Wipes are sheets made from materials like **polyester, polypropylene, cotton, wood pulp or rayon**.
They're moistened with water plus cleansing and moisturising ingredients, and may contain **preservatives** to stop bacteria and mold.

## Cosmetic, drug or something else?
- Wipes for cleansing skin, like **baby care or makeup removal**, are regulated as cosmetics in the US.
- Wipes with medical claims, like **killing germs on skin** or for skin conditions such as acne or diaper rash, count as drugs there.
- Surface-disinfecting wipes and **insect repellent wipes** fall under a different agency.

So read the label for what the wipe is actually for.

## For sensitive skin
Check the **full ingredient list** before using wipes.
FDA notes that some parents have reacted to baby wipes that didn't bother their babies.

Happy shopping!
