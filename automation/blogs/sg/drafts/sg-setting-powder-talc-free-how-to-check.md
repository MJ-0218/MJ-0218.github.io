Hello Singapore!
Setting powder, dry shampoo powder, body powder: our humidity gives them a lot of work.
One ingredient that pops up again and again is **talc**. Here's a quick read.

![Talc or talc-free card](/assets/cards/sg-setting-powder-talc-free-how-to-check-summary.png)

## The short version
Talc is a **mined mineral**.
In cosmetics, the US FDA says it may be used to **absorb moisture**, **prevent caking**, make makeup **opaque** or improve how a product **feels**.

## Does anyone check it before sale?
In the US, cosmetic products and ingredients (except color additives) **don't go through FDA review or approval** before they're sold.
Brands are responsible for safety and proper labelling.

## Why talc gets extra attention
Talc can sit near **asbestos** in the earth, so there's a **potential for contamination**.
That's why the FDA says mining sites need careful selection and the ore needs proper testing.

The FDA has continued testing talc-containing cosmetics for asbestos.
It also withdrew a proposed testing rule on **28 November 2025** for further review, so standard US testing methods are still being worked out.

## Shopping checklist
- Scan the ingredient list for **"talc."**
- "Talc-free" on the front? Confirm it on the **back label** too.
- Prefer listings that show the **complete ingredient list**.

Happy shopping!
