Hi everyone!
The sky has been playing hide-and-seek with the haze this week.
NEA's forecast for **23 October** puts the 24-hr PSI at **101–150, in the Unhealthy range**.

So here's a gentle routine for when you get home.
Nothing fancy, just small habits for your skin, hair and home.

![Hazy week routine card](/assets/cards/sg-haze-day-skin-home-routine-summary.png)

## First, check the right number

For plans later today, NEA says to look at the **1-hr PM2.5** reading.
For tomorrow's plans, use the **24-hr PSI forecast**.

If you're healthy, NEA's advice for 23 Oct is to cut down on long or strenuous outdoor exertion.
Showers are forecast, and NEA expects the haze to improve on Sunday.

## Skin: rinse, soothe, seal

- **Rinse first.** Wash your face with lukewarm water and a gentle cleanser once you're home. No need to scrub hard.
- **Soothe.** If your skin feels tight, a light, fragrance-free moisturiser is usually enough in our humid weather.
- **Seal at night.** Swap to a clean pillowcase a little more often during hazy weeks.

What to look for in a cleanser for this kind of week:
a low-foam or gel texture, no strong fragrance, and a size you'll actually finish.

## Hair: a quick reset

Hair holds on to smells easily.
If you've been out for a long time, a simple rinse and wash in the evening can make you feel fresher before bed.

Tie long hair up when you head out, so less of it is exposed.

## Home: keep the hazy air out

- Close windows during the hazy hours, and open them again when the readings drop.
- If you use an air purifier, check the filter. Filters only work well when they're not overdue for a change.
- Run the aircon in recirculation mode if your unit has one.

Thinking of getting an air purifier?
Look at the room size it's rated for, the filter type it uses, and how much replacement filters cost.

Take it easy this week, stay hydrated, and check the readings before you head out!
