Hi ARMY (and BTS-curious friends)!
If December already felt busy, it's about to get a lot more purple-and-red.
**BTS The City Arirang** is coming to Singapore for the whole month.

![BTS The City Arirang Singapore card](/assets/cards/sg-bts-the-city-arirang-december-2026-summary.png)

## The key dates

The citywide fan experience runs from **1 to 31 December**.
It happens alongside BTS's concerts at the **National Stadium on 17, 19, 20 and 22 December**.

Singapore is the only Southeast Asian stop for this experience.
It's organised with the Singapore Tourism Board and HYBE, BTS's agency.

## Where the fun happens

- **Jewel Changi Airport**: a themed light and music show at the Rain Vortex.
- **Mandai Wildlife Reserve**: a BTS takeover of Exploria, the new immersive multimedia attraction.
- **National Gallery Singapore**: an art trail pairing BTS music with works from *Pop After Pop: Art Around Us*.
- **National Museum of Singapore**: light and sound installations.
- **Universal Studios Singapore**: fireworks choreographed to BTS music.
- **Sentosa**: a themed race and installations.
- **The Kallang**: interactive activity zones.
- **Zouk Singapore**: opening and closing night parties.

From **8 to 22 December**, landmarks around Marina Bay and the Singapore River glow red each night.
Look out for Gardens by the Bay, the Singapore Flyer, The Fullerton Hotel and The Helix Bridge.

## A humid-day concert kit

Concert days in Singapore mean heat, crowds and maybe a surprise shower.
Here's a simple kit to keep you comfortable from queue to encore:

- **A small fan or handheld fan** for the queue.
- **Sweat-resistant sunscreen** if you're queuing outdoors in the afternoon. Look for SPF and PA on the label, and reapply.
- **Blotting papers or a compact powder** to keep your face fresh for photos.
- **A power bank** for tickets, photos and the ride home.
- **A foldable poncho** — umbrellas aren't friendly in crowds.
- **A clear pouch** to keep your lightstick, phone and tissues together.

Check the venue's bag rules before you go, since they can change by event.

More details on The City Arirang are still to come, so keep an eye on official updates.
