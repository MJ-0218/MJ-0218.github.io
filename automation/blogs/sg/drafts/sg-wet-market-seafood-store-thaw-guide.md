Hello Singapore!
Your wet market haul is home. Now what?
Here's a simple store-thaw-cook routine based on US FDA advice.

![Seafood storage card](/assets/cards/sg-wet-market-seafood-store-thaw-guide-summary.png)

## Store it soon
Put seafood on ice, or in the fridge or freezer, soon after buying.

- Cooking within **2 days**? Keep it in a clean fridge at **4°C (40°F) or below**.
- Otherwise, wrap it tightly in plastic, foil or moisture-proof paper and **freeze** it.

## Thaw it the slow, safe way
FDA suggests thawing frozen seafood **in the fridge overnight**.

In a hurry? Seal it in a plastic bag and put it in **cold water**.
You can also use the microwave's defrost setting if you'll cook it right after.

## Keep raw away from ready-to-eat
- Wash your hands for at least **20 seconds** with soap and warm water after handling raw seafood.
- Wash cutting boards, plates, utensils and counters with **soap and hot water** before using them for cooked food.

## Cook it through
Most seafood should reach an internal temperature of **63°C (145°F)**.
No thermometer?

- **Fish:** flesh turns opaque and separates easily with a fork.
- **Prawns, crab:** flesh becomes firm, pearly and opaque.
- **Clams, mussels:** shells open. Throw out any that stay shut.

## Serving
Don't leave seafood out for more than **2 hours**, or more than **1 hour** when it's hotter than 32°C (90°F).

Happy cooking!
