Hello Singapore!
Humid days and frizzy hair go together, so many of us look at keratin or smoothing treatments.
Here's a quick checklist based on US FDA advice before you sit in the chair.

![Salon checklist card](/assets/cards/sg-keratin-treatment-formaldehyde-what-to-ask-summary.png)

## What's the concern?
A smoothing solution is usually applied, then sealed into the hair with heat.
FDA says these products often contain **formaldehyde**, and heating releases it **into the air as a gas**.

## Question 1: What's in it?
Ask your stylist whether the product contains **formaldehyde-related ingredients**.

This matters because products sold **only to salon professionals** may not have an ingredient list.
They are still required to carry directions for safe use and any needed warnings.

## Question 2: Is there a Safety Data Sheet?
In the US, workplace rules require products with hazardous chemicals to have a **Safety Data Sheet (SDS)** listing their hazards.
It's fair to ask whether the salon has one for the product.

## Question 3: Is the room ventilated?
FDA notes that in a poorly ventilated salon, both stylists and clients risk inhaling the released formaldehyde.

## If the answer is "not sure"
FDA says you may not want to use the product, especially if you've reacted before.

## Buying a home kit?
Check the ingredient list for **formaldehyde, formalin or methylene glycol**.
No ingredient list at all? FDA recommends not buying it.

Smooth hair is nice, but knowing what's in the bottle is better!
