Hi everyone!
With dengue in the news again, it's a good week to look at the small corners of home where water sits.
A flowerpot plate here, a forgotten pail there… that's all mosquitoes need.

Here's a quick routine based on the US CDC's mosquito checklist.
Set a weekly reminder on your phone and it only takes a short walk around the flat.

![Weekly water check card](/assets/cards/sg-mosquito-proof-home-weekly-check-summary.png)

## The weekly check

CDC says to check for water-holding containers indoors and outdoors.
**Once a week**, empty and scrub, turn over, cover or throw out things that hold water.

Walk through these spots:

- **Flowerpot saucers** on the balcony or windowsill
- **Pails and buckets** in the toilet or service yard
- **Toys** and small containers on the balcony
- **Pet water bowls** (change and scrub them)
- **Trash bins** with lids that collect rain

Scrubbing matters, not just emptying.
That's the step people skip.

## Keep them outside

CDC suggests screens on windows and doors, and fixing any holes in them.
Running the aircon, if you have one, also helps keep mosquitoes away.

## When you're out at the void deck or park

Use an insect repellent registered with the US EPA, or one with the same active ingredients: DEET, picaridin, IR3535, oil of lemon eucalyptus (OLE) or PMD.
Wearing sunscreen too? Put sunscreen on first, repellent second, CDC says.

Loose, long-sleeved tops and long pants help in the evening, when you're near greenery.

## Buying tip: screens and repellents

For window screens, measure the window frame first and check whether the mesh can be removed for cleaning.
For repellents, read the active ingredient and how long the label says it lasts, then choose what fits your day.

Do your quick check this weekend, and stay bite-free!
