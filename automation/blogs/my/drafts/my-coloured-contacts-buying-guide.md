Hello hello!
Coloured lenses are a quick way to change up a look, for Halloween or just a weekend outing.
But not every pair online is a good idea. Here's a buyer's checklist built on the US FDA's guidance.

![Coloured lens buying card](/assets/cards/my-coloured-contacts-buying-guide-summary.png)

## First, know what you're buying

FDA says decorative contact lenses are **medical devices**, just like vision-correcting lenses.
That's why fit, cleanliness and the seller all matter.

Rules differ by country, so ask an optometrist in Malaysia what applies to you.

## Where not to buy

FDA warns against buying lenses from:

- Street vendors
- Beauty supply stores and novelty shops
- Flea markets
- Unknown online sellers

Lenses from these places may be contaminated or counterfeit, FDA says.

## What to check before buying

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Eye exam first | A wrong fit can damage your eyes |
| Brand name and lens measurements | So you get lenses that match your eyes |
| Expiry date on the pack | Expired products aren't worth the risk |
| Care instructions included | You need clear wearing and cleaning steps |

## While wearing them

- Follow the wearing, cleaning and disinfecting instructions.
- Never share lenses. In the FDA's words, you wouldn't share your toothbrush either.

## When to take them out

Redness, eye pain that doesn't go away, decreased vision or discharge can be signs of infection.
FDA says to remove the lenses and see an eye doctor right away.

Jom, choose carefully and keep those eyes happy!
