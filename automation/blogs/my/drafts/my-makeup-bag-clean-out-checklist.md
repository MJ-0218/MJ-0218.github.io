Hello hello!
Weekend project idea: empty your makeup bag onto the table and be honest with yourself.
Some of those products have been with you longer than your phone, right?

Here's a simple clean-out checklist, based on the US FDA's eye cosmetic safety tips.

![Makeup bag clean-out card](/assets/cards/my-makeup-bag-clean-out-checklist-summary.png)

## Step 1: Toss pile

Put these straight in the bin:

- **Mascara older than a few months.** FDA says manufacturers usually recommend discarding mascara 2 to 4 months after purchase.
- **Dried-up mascara.** Don't revive it with water or saliva. FDA explains that this can bring in bacteria and weaken the preservative.
- **Eye products you used during an eye infection.** FDA says to discard them.
- **Anything that irritates your eyes.** Stop using it. If irritation continues, see a doctor.

## Step 2: Keep pile, but clean it

Wipe down the outside of tubes and palettes.
FDA advises keeping containers clean and not letting cosmetics get covered in dust or dirt.

Wash brushes and check that any tool going near your eyes is clean.

## Step 3: Pick a cooler home

FDA says **not to store cosmetics above 85°F**, about 29°C.
In a parked car in KL, that's easy to pass, so don't leave your pouch there.

A drawer in an air-conditioned room is a good spot.
Avoid the windowsill and the shelf right above a hot shower.

## Step 4: New rules for the new bag

- No sharing eye makeup, even with your sister. FDA warns that another person's germs may be hazardous.
- At beauty counters, ask for single-use applicators like clean cotton swabs.
- Use each product only where it is meant to go, so keep the lip pencil away from your lash line.

## Buying replacements

Smaller sizes make sense for products you need to replace often, like mascara.
Read the ingredient list on the pack, too. FDA notes retail cosmetics must declare their ingredients.

Happy decluttering, and jom enjoy that fresh, tidy makeup bag!
