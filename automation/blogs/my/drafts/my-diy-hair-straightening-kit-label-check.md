Hello hello!
At-home rebonding and keratin kits are everywhere online, often cheaper than a salon visit.
Before you add one to cart, here's a label check based on the US FDA.

![DIY smoothing kit card](/assets/cards/my-diy-hair-straightening-kit-label-check-summary.png)

## Rule one: no ingredient list, no buy
FDA says hair smoothing products sold at retail, including online, **must list their ingredients**.
If there's no list, FDA recommends you **don't buy it**.

## The names to search for
Zoom in on the product photos or description and look for:

- Formaldehyde
- Formalin
- Methylene glycol

FDA notes these smoothing products often contain formaldehyde under one of these names.

## Why heat is the tricky part
These kits work by applying a solution, then sealing it in with heat.
That heat releases formaldehyde **into the air as a gas**, so a small, closed bathroom isn't a great place to do it.

## Who should be extra careful
Some people react strongly to formaldehyde while others don't react at all, FDA says.
Repeated contact can also lead to skin sensitization, even if earlier uses felt fine.

If you've had a reaction before, consider skipping these products altogether.

## If something goes wrong
Stinging eyes, coughing or a rash after use? Rinse, get fresh air, and talk to your doctor.
