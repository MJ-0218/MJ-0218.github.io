Hi everyone!
If your group chats have been buzzing about Waze and a new app called TuJu, you're not alone.
Here's a calm, simple rundown of what was actually said, and what it means for your next drive.

![Waze and TuJu summary card](/assets/cards/my-waze-tuju-switch-2026-summary.png)

## What did the government say?

The government is encouraging Malaysians to uninstall Waze and consider a local navigation app instead.
It comes down to data: Waze is subject to Israeli law, so users' personal and movement data could fall under that jurisdiction.

The suggested local option is TuJu.
If you're curious, it's worth knowing what it is before you switch.

## So what is TuJu?

TuJu is a highway navigation app developed by the Malaysian Highway Authority (LLM) together with Prosignal Consortium Sdn Bhd.
It was launched in November 2025 and gives traffic updates along with highway information.

The Prime Minister suggested it as a local alternative earlier this week, and lots of people downloaded it at once.
With so many new users arriving together, a little patience in the first few days goes a long way.

## Thinking of switching? A gentle checklist

- **Try it on a route you know first.** Your daily commute is the easiest way to see if the directions feel right.
- **Keep a backup for long trips.** Until you're comfortable, it's okay to have a second map app ready for balik kampung drives.
- **Check app permissions.** Whatever app you use, look at location settings and choose "while using the app" if you prefer.
- **Update before you drive.** New versions often fix bugs, so update it at home on Wi-Fi.
- **Set it up before moving.** Type your destination while parked, never while driving.

## Before you delete anything

Switching apps is easier when you don't rush it.
Save your home and office addresses in the new app first, then test it on a short trip.

If you share rides or drive for work, tell your regular passengers or colleagues which app you're using.
That way nobody is confused by a different arrival time on the screen.

Drive safe, stay patient in the jam, and have a lovely week!
