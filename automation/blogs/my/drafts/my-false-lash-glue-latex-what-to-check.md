Hi everyone!
Planning a glam look for a wedding or a costume party?
If you use false lashes, the glue is worth a closer look.

![False lash glue card](/assets/cards/my-false-lash-glue-latex-what-to-check-summary.png)

## Why lash glue?
The US FDA names **eyelash adhesives** among products that commonly contain **natural rubber latex**, along with body paints and hair bonding glue.

## What the label must show
There's **no required latex warning** on cosmetics.
But labels must include an **ingredient list**, so that's where to look.

## Spot these words
Natural Rubber Latex, Natural Latex, Rubber Latex, Latex Rubber, Natural Liquid Latex, Aqueous Latex or Adhesive Latex all **mean latex**.

## Don't skip the re-check
Ingredients **can change anytime**.
Check the list again whenever you buy, even for a product you've used before.

## If you get a reaction
**Stop using it** and contact a healthcare professional.

Take care!
