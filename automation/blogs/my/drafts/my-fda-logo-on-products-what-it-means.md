Hi everyone!
Some product photos online show an FDA logo right on the box or banner.
According to the US FDA, that deserves a second look.

![FDA logo card](/assets/cards/my-fda-logo-on-products-what-it-means-summary.png)

## The logo isn't a seal of approval
FDA says its logo is for **official government use only**.
It shouldn't be used to suggest the agency **endorses** a private company, product or service, and misuse may violate US federal law.

## What the US FDA doesn't approve
- **Cosmetics** like makeup, moisturisers, shampoo and hair dye. The exception is color additives.
- **Dietary supplements**, for safety and effectiveness.
- Individual **food labels**, including the Nutrition Facts panel.

So a cream or vitamin with an "FDA approved" badge is making a claim the US FDA itself doesn't support.

## What to look at instead
- The full ingredient list
- Clear directions and warnings
- Who made it and where

Keep in mind this is the **US** agency's view. Malaysia has its own regulator and rules for cosmetics and supplements, so check those as well.

Happy shopping!
