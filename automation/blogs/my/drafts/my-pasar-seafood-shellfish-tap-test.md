Hi everyone!
Lala, kerang and ketam are favourites at home.
Shellfish spoil fast, so here's the US FDA's checklist before you buy.

![Shellfish card](/assets/cards/my-pasar-seafood-shellfish-tap-test-summary.png)

## Clams, mussels and oysters
- Throw away any with **cracked or broken shells**.
- Do the **tap test**: live ones close when you tap the shell. If they stay open, leave them.

## Crabs
Live crabs should show **some leg movement**.
FDA says crabs spoil quickly after they die, so pick live ones to cook.

## Prawns
The flesh should be **clear and pearl-like**, with little or no smell.

## Frozen seafood packs
- Skip packs that are **open, torn or crushed** at the edges.
- Avoid **frost or ice crystals**, which can mean it was stored too long or thawed and refrozen.
- The fish should be **hard**, not bendable.

## Cooking signs
- Clams and mussels: the **shells open**. Throw out any that don't.
- Crab and prawns: the flesh turns **firm, pearly and opaque**.

## One more thing
Cooked seafood from a stall should be **kept separate** from raw seafood, in its own case or behind a divider.
