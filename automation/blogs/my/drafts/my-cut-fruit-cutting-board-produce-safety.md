Hello hello!
Fruit platters, ulam-ulaman and salads are eaten raw, so how you prep them matters.
Here are the US FDA's key tips.

![Produce prep card](/assets/cards/my-cut-fruit-cutting-board-produce-safety-summary.png)

## Keep raw meat away
FDA says to keep fruits and vegetables that will be eaten raw **separate from raw meat, poultry and seafood**.
If possible, use **one cutting board for produce** and a separate one for raw meat.

## Clean between jobs
Wash boards, knives and countertops with **soap and hot water** between preparing raw meat and produce that won't be cooked.

The soap is for your **tools**, not the fruit.

## Wash the produce, no soap
- Rinse all produce **under running water**.
- FDA does **not** recommend soap, detergent or produce wash on fruits and vegetables.
- Scrub firm ones like **melons and cucumbers** with a clean brush, then dry.

## Keep cut fruit cold
FDA says to refrigerate **all produce bought pre-cut or packaged**.
Perishables like strawberries, lettuce, herbs and mushrooms belong in a fridge at **4°C or below**.

## Quick check
- Cut away bruised spots.
- Throw out anything that looks rotten.
- Wash your hands for at least **20 seconds** before and after.
