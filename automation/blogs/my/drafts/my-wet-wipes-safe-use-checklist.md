Hi everyone!
From mamak wipe-downs to makeup removal, wet wipes are part of daily life.
Here's a short checklist based on US FDA advice.

![Wet wipe checklist card](/assets/cards/my-wet-wipes-safe-use-checklist-summary.png)

## 6 everyday habits
1. Use wipes only on **unbroken, intact skin**.
2. Use them only for their **intended use**, and follow the label.
3. Let your skin **dry thoroughly** afterward.
4. Keep the pack **tightly closed** so wipes don't dry out.
5. **Throw away used wipes** right away to prevent cross contamination.
6. Check the label before flushing. Some wipes are labelled flushable, but **not all** of them.

## Watch the heat
Wipes left in a **hot car** can dry out, and heat can change their ingredients.
The preservatives that fight bacteria and mold may break down.

## Stop if you notice this
- **Rash**, raised red bumps, redness, irritation or burning where the wipe touched you. Stop and see a doctor.
- An **off color or bad smell**. FDA says it wants to know about these too.

FDA also notes that some wipes have been **recalled** for bacteria or mold contamination.

Take care!
