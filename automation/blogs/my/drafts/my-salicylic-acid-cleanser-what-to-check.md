Hi everyone!
Salicylic acid shows up in so many cleansers and toners now.
Here's a short guide to buying it smartly, based on the US FDA's notes.

![Salicylic acid card](/assets/cards/my-salicylic-acid-cleanser-what-to-check-summary.png)

## How it shows up on labels
Salicylic acid is the **most common BHA** in cosmetics.
Related names on a label can include **salicylate**, **sodium salicylate** and **willow extract**.

## Look at the directions too
An industry safety panel said products with salicylic acid should either contain sunscreen or tell you to use **other sun protection**.
So a good label should mention sun care.

## The FDA's precautions
- **Patch test** before using it on a large area.
- Don't go beyond the **recommended applications**.
- Stop if you get irritation or **prolonged stinging**, and see a doctor.
- **Avoid** BHA products for **infants and children**.
- Wear **sun protection** while using BHA.

## When buying online
Pick listings that show the **full ingredient list** and the usage directions.

Take care!
