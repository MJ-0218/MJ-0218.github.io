Hello hello!
Home gel kits and cute polish shades are everywhere in online sales right now.
Before your next DIY session, here are five quick rules from the US FDA's nail product guide.

![Home nail rules card](/assets/cards/my-nail-salon-home-mani-safety-summary.png)

## Rule 1: The label is the manual

FDA says to read nail product labels carefully and follow any warnings.
Retail cosmetics must also list their ingredients, so you can check for anything that bothered you before.

## Rule 2: Let the air move

FDA's tip: **use nail products where there's good ventilation**.
A fan by an open window works better than a sealed room with the aircon on.

## Rule 3: No flames, no hot tools

Some nail products catch fire easily, FDA warns, near a stove's pilot light, a lit cigarette or the heating element of a curling iron.
So paint your nails away from the kitchen, and switch off hair tools first.

## Rule 4: Mind the eyes and the kids

Nail products can be dangerous if they get in your eyes.
Some ingredients can also be harmful if swallowed, so keep bottles out of children's reach.

## Rule 5: Stop if your skin complains

Infections and allergic reactions can happen with some nail products, FDA notes.
Redness, itching or swelling around the nail? Stop using the product, and see a doctor if it doesn't settle.

## Shopping tip

Compare ingredient lists between brands if you've reacted to something before.
For colours you rarely wear, mini bottles save money and space.

Happy painting, and jom keep it safe!
