Hi everyone!
That little lens case gets used every day, so it needs a bit of love too.
Here's how the US FDA says to look after it.

![Lens case card](/assets/cards/my-contact-lens-case-replace-3-months-summary.png)

## Why the case matters
FDA says contact lens cases can be a **source of bacterial growth**.

## After every use
1. Using clean fingers, **rub and rinse** the case with fresh multi-purpose solution.
2. **Flip it over** and shake out the extra solution.
3. Dry it with a fresh, clean cloth, or let it **air-dry**.

Never rinse it with tap water. FDA says lenses shouldn't be exposed to any water.

## Replace it on time
Change your case **every 3 months**, or as your eye doctor tells you.

## While you're at it
- Throw away old solution in the case after each use, and don't top it off.
- Wash your hands before handling lenses.
- Red or irritated eyes? Remove the lenses and see your eye doctor.

Take care of those eyes!
