Hi everyone!
With our weather, smooth and manageable hair is the dream, so rebonding and smoothing treatments are everywhere.
Let's go through what the US FDA says before your next appointment.

![Rebonding card](/assets/cards/my-hair-rebonding-formaldehyde-ventilation-summary.png)

## How these treatments work
A straightening or smoothing solution goes on the hair, then heat seals it in.
FDA says these products often contain **formaldehyde**, also known as **formalin** and **methylene glycol**.

## Why ventilation matters
Heating the solution releases formaldehyde **into the air as a gas**.
If the salon is not properly ventilated, **both the stylist and the client** are at risk of breathing it in.

Formaldehyde is a colourless, strong-smelling gas.
FDA says it's a health hazard when inhaled or when it gets into the eyes or onto the skin.

## Reactions that have been reported
- Eye problems or irritation
- Headaches and dizziness
- Sore or scratchy throat, cough, wheezing
- Nausea, chest pain, vomiting and rash

Repeated contact can also lead to skin sensitisation or allergic dermatitis.

## Your quick checks
- Retail product? Look for **formaldehyde, formalin, methylene glycol** on the ingredient list.
- No ingredient list at all? FDA says to skip that product.
- At a salon? Ask whether the product has formaldehyde-related ingredients.

Got a bad reaction? See your doctor and report it.
Take care of your hair and your lungs!
