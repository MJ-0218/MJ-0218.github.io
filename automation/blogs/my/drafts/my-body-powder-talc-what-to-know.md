Hi everyone!
In weather like ours, a dab of body powder after a shower feels so good.
If you've wondered about the **talc** in it, here are four facts straight from the US FDA.

![Body powder talc card](/assets/cards/my-body-powder-talc-what-to-know-summary.png)

## 1. It's a mineral from the ground
Talc is a **naturally occurring mineral** that's mined.
You'll find it in many cosmetics, from **baby powder to blush**.

## 2. It's there to keep things dry
Talc may be used to **absorb moisture** and **prevent caking**.
It can also make makeup more opaque and improve its feel.

## 3. Contamination is the main worry
Talc and asbestos can be found **close together** in the earth.
So the FDA stresses choosing mining sites carefully and testing the ore well.

## 4. The FDA still tests products
The FDA has **continued testing** talc-containing cosmetics for asbestos.

## Before you add to cart
- Check the **ingredients** for "talc."
- Want to avoid it? Look for **talc-free** and confirm in the list.
- Buy from sellers that show the **full ingredient list**.

Take care!
