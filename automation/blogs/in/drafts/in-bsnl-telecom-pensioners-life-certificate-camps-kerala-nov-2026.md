Hello everyone!
This one's for BSNL and telecom pensioners in Kerala, or anyone helping a parent with their pension paperwork.

Special camps are coming up to renew your **life certificate**.
They're part of the nationwide **Digital Life Certificate Campaign 5.0**.

![BSNL pensioners camp card](/assets/cards/in-bsnl-telecom-pensioners-life-certificate-camps-kerala-nov-2026-summary.png)

## Who these camps are for
The camps are meant for BSNL/telecom pensioners whose life certificate **has expired** or is **due to expire** in **September, October, November or December 2026**.

So if your renewal month falls anywhere in that window, these camps are for you.

## One-day camps in four cities
Every one-day camp runs from **10 am to 2 pm**.

| City | Venue | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Kollam | BSNL Telephone Bhavan, Vellayittambalam | 9 Nov 2026 |
| Ernakulam | BSNL Bhavan, Ernakulam South, Kochi | 10 Nov 2026 |
| Kozhikode | BSNL, Ashokapuram | 16 Nov 2026 |
| Palakkad | BSNL CTO Building, near the Head Post Office | 17 Nov 2026 |

## The month-long camp in Thiruvananthapuram
There's also a longer camp at the **CCA Kerala Office**, Door Sanchar Bhavan (BSNL Circle Office), **PMG Junction, Thiruvananthapuram**.

It runs from **1 to 30 November 2026**, but **not on Saturdays and Sundays**.
Timings here are a little longer: **10 am to 3.30 pm**.

## Who's organising it
The camps are run by the **Office of the Controller of Communication Accounts (CCA), Kerala**, Thiruvananthapuram.
For more details, check the CCA Kerala website at **cgca.gov.in/ccakl**.

## Small tips for the day
Note the date for your city on the calendar now, so it doesn't slip by.
And if you're accompanying an elderly parent, plan the trip so they don't have to wait standing for long.

Take care, and pass this on to anyone in the family who needs it!
