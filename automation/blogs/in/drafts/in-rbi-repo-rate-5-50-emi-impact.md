Hello friends! If you have a home loan or car loan, this one matters to your monthly budget.
The Reserve Bank of India has **raised the repo rate** by 25 basis points.

Here is a simple breakdown, straight from the RBI's official minutes.

![RBI repo rate card](/assets/cards/in-rbi-repo-rate-5-50-emi-impact-summary.png)

## What RBI decided
The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) met from **5 to 7 October 2026**.
All members voted to raise the policy repo rate by **25 basis points to 5.50 per cent**.

Two related rates moved with it:

- Standing deposit facility (SDF) rate: **5.25 per cent**
- Marginal standing facility (MSF) rate and Bank Rate: **5.75 per cent**

The MPC also changed its stance to **"calibrated tightening"**.

## Why RBI raised rates
CPI inflation rose to **4.8 per cent in August 2026**, from 4.5 per cent in July.
RBI now projects inflation at **5.2 per cent** for 2026-27, citing a deficient monsoon, El Niño conditions and high energy prices.

Growth still looks steady. RBI projects real GDP growth of **7.1 per cent** for 2026-27.

## What it can mean for your EMI (example)
How much your EMI changes depends on your bank and loan type.
Here is a simple example if your loan rate goes up by the same 25 basis points:

- Loan: ₹30 lakh for 20 years
- At 8.50%: EMI about **₹26,035**
- At 8.75%: EMI about **₹26,511**

That is roughly **₹477 more per month**, or about ₹5,700 a year.
Ask your bank whether your loan rate is linked to the repo rate and when it resets.

## For savers
Higher policy rates can lift deposit rates over time, but each bank decides its own FD rates.
Compare a few banks before you renew a fixed deposit.

## What's next
The next MPC meeting is scheduled for **2 to 4 December 2026**.
We'll update you when the decision is out. Take care, everyone!
