Hello friends!
Mark your calendar: one Tuesday in November is going to be very special this year.

**Guru Nanak Jayanti, Dev Diwali and Kartik Purnima all fall on 24 November 2026.**
Here's what that means for your plans, including whether your bank will be open.

![24 November 2026 festivals card](/assets/cards/in-guru-nanak-jayanti-dev-diwali-2026-november-24-summary.png)

## Three festivals, one day
**Guru Nanak Jayanti (Gurpurab)** marks the birth anniversary of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, the founder of Sikhism and the first Sikh Guru.
Gurdwaras hold prayers, kirtan, processions and langar.

**Kartik Purnima** is the full moon day of the month of Kartik.
Many devotees take a holy dip in rivers, light lamps and give in charity.

**Dev Diwali**, the "Diwali of the gods", is celebrated on Kartik Purnima.
It's best known in **Varanasi**, where the Ganga ghats glow with thousands of diyas.

## Bank holiday on 24 November: which cities?
RBI's November 2026 list marks **Guru Nanak Jayanti / Karthika Purnima** on Tuesday, 24 November.
Banks will be closed that day in these **21 centres**:

Aizawl, Belapur, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Chandigarh, Dehradun, Hyderabad, Itanagar, Jaipur, Jammu, Kanpur, Kohima, Kolkata, Lucknow, Mumbai, Nagpur, New Delhi, Raipur, Ranchi, Shimla and Srinagar.

If your city isn't on the list, your branch should be open as usual.

## Planning a Varanasi trip for Dev Diwali?
Dev Diwali draws huge crowds to the ghats every year.
Book your train, flight and stay early, and plan for heavy foot traffic near the river in the evening.

## Other dates around it
- **20 November**: Devutthana Ekadashi
- **21 November**: Tulsi Vivah

Festival dates can shift by a day depending on your regional panchang, so do check locally.

Wishing you a peaceful Gurpurab and a bright Dev Diwali!
