Namaste farmer friends!
If you're planning your rabi sowing, here are the new **Minimum Support Prices (MSP)** for the **Rabi Marketing Season (RMS) 2027-28**.

The government approved higher MSPs for all mandated rabi crops on **30 September 2026**.

![Rabi MSP card](/assets/cards/in-rabi-msp-2027-28-wheat-mustard-list-summary.png)

## Rabi MSP 2027-28: full list
All prices are in **₹ per quintal**.

| Crop | MSP 2027-28 | MSP 2026-27 | Increase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wheat | 2,610 | 2,585 | +25 |
| Barley | 2,286 | 2,150 | +136 |
| Gram | 5,958 | 5,875 | +83 |
| Lentil (Masur) | 7,390 | 7,000 | +390 |
| Rapeseed & Mustard | 6,613 | 6,200 | +413 |
| Safflower | 7,215 | 6,540 | +675 |

## Which crop got the biggest hike?
**Safflower** gets the biggest jump at **₹675** per quintal.
Next come **rapeseed & mustard** (₹413), **masur** (₹390) and **barley** (₹136).

Wheat goes up by just **₹25**, to ₹2,610.

## Margin over cost
The government says MSP keeps a **minimum margin of 50%** over the cost of production.

- Wheat: **106%** over cost
- Rapeseed & mustard: **96%**
- Masur: **92%**
- Gram: **59%**
- Barley: **58%**
- Safflower: **50%**

## How much money is involved
The government estimates about **₹90,962 crore** in MSP payments to farmers for **324 lakh metric tonnes** of produce in RMS 2027-28.

## Who buys at MSP
- **Wheat and other cereals:** the **Food Corporation of India (FCI)** and designated State agencies.
- **Pulses and oilseeds** (gram, masur, mustard, safflower): under the **Price Support Scheme (PSS)** of **PM-AASHA**, through **NAFED** and **NCCF**. PSS kicks in when market prices fall below MSP.

## How to register for PSS procurement
NAFED's **e-Samriddhi** and NCCF's **e-Samyukti** portals handle registration, procurement and payment.

1. Register with your **Aadhaar, land records, bank account** and crop details.
2. Pick your **nearest procurement centre**. The portal gives you a visit date.
3. Payment goes **directly to your bank account**.

Since the **2025-26 season**, **biometric authentication** is mandatory under PSS.
It's done on an Aadhaar-enabled PoS machine or by face authentication before procurement.

Produce must meet **Fair Average Quality (FAQ)** standards.
Your State and the procurement agencies publish centre locations and schedules, so keep an eye on local notices.

Wishing you a great rabi season!
