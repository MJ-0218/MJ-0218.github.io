Big news for anyone who follows banking in India.
The Government has set up a **High Level Committee on Banking for Viksit Bharat** to review the whole banking sector.

Nothing changes for your bank account today.
But what this committee recommends could shape how banks work in the years ahead.

![Banking committee card](/assets/cards/in-high-level-committee-banking-viksit-bharat-members-tor-summary.png)

## Why it was set up
Its job is to recommend **structural, institutional, regulatory and legislative reforms** to make Indian banking **globally competitive, financially inclusive and resilient**, with **Viksit Bharat 2047** as the goal.

Finance Minister **Nirmala Sitharaman** had announced the committee in her **Budget speech for 2026-27**.

## Who's on it
- **Chairman:** Suresh N. Patel, former Central Vigilance Commissioner
- **Member:** Shirish Chandra Murmu, Deputy Governor, RBI
- **Member:** Padmaja Chunduru, former MD & CEO, Indian Bank
- **Member:** N S Vishwanathan, former Deputy Governor, RBI
- **Member Secretary:** Joint Secretary (Banking), Department of Financial Services

## How long it has
The committee's tenure is **six months**.

## What it will look at
Here are its terms of reference, in plain words:

- **Banking practices and prudential norms:** compare with global best practices and give a roadmap for globally competitive banks.
- **Rules and laws for banks:** including **Regional Rural Banks and Cooperative Banks**, to improve efficiency and ease of doing banking.
- **Long-term capital needs:** for banks, **Development Financial Institutions and NBFCs**, and how to fund credit growth.
- **Cost of capital:** policy options to boost private investment.
- **AI and digital banking:** a roadmap with **safeguards against cyber risks**.
- **People:** a plan for hiring and training a future-ready banking workforce.
- **Priority Sector Lending and the Lead Bank Scheme:** check if they still work well for inclusive credit.
- Anything else the Government refers to it.

## Why it matters to regular customers
Two items stand out for everyday users: the **AI and cyber-safety roadmap**, and the review of **priority sector lending**, which the committee will check for inclusive credit delivery.
