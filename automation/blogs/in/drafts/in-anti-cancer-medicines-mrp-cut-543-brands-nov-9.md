Hello friends!
If someone in your family is on cancer treatment, this one is worth a read.

The government's drug price regulator has cut the MRP of **543 brands of anti-cancer medicines**.
The new prices kick in on **November 9, 2026**.

![Anti-cancer MRP card](/assets/cards/in-anti-cancer-medicines-mrp-cut-543-brands-nov-9-summary.png)

## What was announced
The **National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA)** released the list on **October 28, 2026**.
NPPA works under the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers.

It covers revised **Maximum Retail Prices (MRP)** for 543 brands, based on prices companies submitted up to **October 27, 2026**.

## Why prices are falling
On **October 17, 2026**, NPPA announced **Trade Margin Rationalisation (TMR)** for **137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs**.
The aim, in NPPA's words, is to address excessive trade margins and make cancer treatment more affordable.

Companies were told to send in their revised prices.
This list of 543 brands is the result.

## Old vs new MRP: a few examples
These are some of the brands NPPA highlighted.

| Medicine (brand) | Pack | Old MRP (₹) | New MRP (₹) | Cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anastrozole 1 mg (ANARZOLE) | 10 tablets | 695.76 | 73.00 | ~90% |
| Azacitidine 100 mg inj. (Azacytin) | 1 | 6,337.00 | 1,495.04 | ~76% |
| Bevacizumab 100 mg/4 ml (BEVACIZAB) | 4 ml vial | 21,767.70 | 5,930.13 | ~73% |
| Dasatinib 20 mg (Dasanat 20) | 60 tablets | 2,812.50 | 1,085.93 | ~61% |
| Denosumab 60 mg (Debrumab) | 1 ml PFS | 22,031.25 | 5,941.00 | ~73% |
| Enzalutamide 40 mg (Enuta) | 28 capsules | 6,578.20 | 2,232.00 | ~66% |
| Erlotinib 150 mg (ERLIB) | 30 tablets | 4,637.45 | 2,008.00 | ~57% |
| Fludarabine 50 mg inj. (FLUDANOX) | vial | 14,235.00 | 2,432.00 | ~83% |
| Fulvestrant 50 mg inj. (Fulbreca) | 5 ml vial | 26,072.16 | 5,491.00 | ~79% |

The percentage cut is our own rounding from NPPA's old and new prices.

## Which medicines are covered
The 137 drugs under the trade margin cap include:

- **Immunotherapy medicines** used for more than 20 types of cancer
- **Denosumab**, used by breast and prostate cancer patients
- **Pegfilgrastim**, used to prevent neutropenia in cancer patients
- **Bevacizumab**, approved for colon, ovarian and cervical cancers

## How to check your brand
NPPA has put the full list of 543 brands on its portal.
It says the portal will be **updated on a continual basis** as more manufacturers submit revised prices.

So if your brand isn't on the list yet, check again later.

## A few practical tips
- The new MRP applies **from November 9, 2026**, not before.
- Compare the MRP printed on the pack with the revised list when you buy.
- Please don't switch brands or change a dose on your own. Talk to your oncologist first.
