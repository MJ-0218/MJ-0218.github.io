Hello friends! Diwali 2026 falls on **Sunday, 8 November**.
The main Lakshmi Puja is that evening, and the festival week begins with Dhanteras on Friday, 6 November.

![Diwali 2026 date summary card](/assets/cards/in-diwali-2026-date-summary.png)

## The five days of Diwali 2026
Five days, one big celebration. Here's how the week lines up this year.

![Diwali 2026 festival days table](/assets/cards/in-diwali-2026-date-days.png)

- **Dhanteras**: Friday, 6 November. A popular day to buy gold, silver or new utensils.
- **Naraka Chaturdashi (Choti Diwali)**: 8 November in much of India. A few regional calendars mark it a day earlier, so follow your family's panchang.
- **Diwali / Lakshmi Puja**: Sunday, 8 November.
- **Govardhan Puja**: 9 or 10 November, depending on the calendar you follow.
- **Bhai Dooj**: Wednesday, 11 November.

## Why we celebrate Diwali

<!-- img:diya -->
Diwali marks the win of good over evil and light over darkness.
For many Hindu families, it's the day Lord Rama returned to Ayodhya after his exile and his victory over Ravana.

It's also tied to Goddess Lakshmi, who stands for wealth and prosperity, and to Lord Krishna's victory over the demon Narakasura.
That's where the name Naraka Chaturdashi comes from.

The festival means different things across communities, too.
Jains mark the nirvana of Lord Mahavira, and Sikhs observe Bandi Chhor Divas around the same time.
Different stories. One shared hope: a fresh, bright start.

## When to do Lakshmi Puja
Lakshmi Puja is done in the evening, during Pradosh Kaal, the time around sunset.
The exact muhurat changes from city to city.

So please don't copy a time you saw for another city.
Open your local panchang, enter your city, and plan the puja around that slot.
Simple, and it saves a lot of last-minute confusion when the whole family is waiting with the thali ready.

## Diwali on a Sunday: is there a holiday?
The central government's 2026 list of closed holidays shows Diwali (Deepavali) on Sunday, 8 November.
Since it's already a Sunday, it doesn't add a weekday off for central government offices. A small bummer, we know.

Schools and banks follow their own state lists.
Some states give a longer Diwali break, so check your school circular or your state's holiday list.

## One more November holiday to note
Guru Nanak Jayanti (Guru Nanak's Birthday) is on **Tuesday, 24 November**.
It's on the central government's closed holiday list too, so plan any bank or office work around it.

## Quick checklist before Diwali
- Fix your puja time using your own city's panchang.
- If you're buying gold or silver on Dhanteras, keep the bill and check the hallmark.
- Book train or bus tickets early if you're travelling home.
- Keep a bucket of water nearby when you light diyas or crackers.

Wishing you and your family a bright, safe and happy Diwali!
