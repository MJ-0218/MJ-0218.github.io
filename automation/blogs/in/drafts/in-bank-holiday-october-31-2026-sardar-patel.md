## Who gets a holiday on 31 October
In the RBI's October 2026 holiday list, **31 October** is marked as a holiday only for **Ahmedabad**.
The occasion is **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's Birthday**.

No other RBI centre in the list shows 31 October as a bank holiday.

## Late October at a glance
The same RBI list also had these holidays this week:
- **Monday, 26 October:** Agartala, Bengaluru, Chandigarh, Jammu, Shimla and Srinagar.
- **Thursday, 29 October:** Karva Chauth in Shimla.

## What still works
Bank holidays close branches, but you can still use **UPI, net banking, mobile banking and ATMs**.
If you need a branch in Ahmedabad, plan for another working day.
