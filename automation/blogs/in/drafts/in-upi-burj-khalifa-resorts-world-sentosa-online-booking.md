Planning a Dubai or Singapore trip?
Here's one less thing to worry about: paying for tickets.

You can now book some of the biggest attractions there **online with your UPI app**, straight from India.

![UPI bookings abroad card](/assets/cards/in-upi-burj-khalifa-resorts-world-sentosa-online-booking-summary.png)

## Where you can now pay with UPI online

**UAE**
- **At the Top, Burj Khalifa**: the first UAE attraction to take UPI for online bookings.
- This was set up with **NEOPAY** and **Emaar Entertainment**.

**Singapore**
- **Resorts World Sentosa** attractions, including **Universal Studios Singapore**, the **Singapore Oceanarium** and **Adventure Cove Waterpark**.
- This was set up with payment firm **FOMO Pay**.

## Who made it happen
**NPCI International Payments Limited (NIPL)**, the international arm of NPCI, announced these tie-ups.

The UAE partnership came on **29 July 2026** and the Singapore one on **9 September 2026**.

## Why this is handy
The government says the move removes **currency friction** for Indian travellers.
In simple words, you're paying the way you already pay every day, and it's one less card to dig out.

Singapore welcomed **1.2 million Indian visitors in 2025**, so this one will be useful for a lot of families.

## Countries where UPI already works
UPI is accepted in **ten countries**:

- Singapore
- UAE
- France
- Mauritius
- Nepal
- Bhutan
- Qatar
- Sri Lanka
- Cambodia
- Greece

**Uzbekistan** is set to become the **11th**, after a commercial agreement announced on **30 August 2026**.

## Remember the Eiffel Tower?
UPI ticketing for the **Eiffel Tower** went live on **26 January 2024**.
These new bookings build on that, so it's clear the list keeps growing.

## Quick tips before you pay
- Check your UPI app's settings for **international payments**, and switch it on if it's off.
- Book from the **official attraction website** only.
- Keep the booking confirmation handy on your phone.

Happy travels!
