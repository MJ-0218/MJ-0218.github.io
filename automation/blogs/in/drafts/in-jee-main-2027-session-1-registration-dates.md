Hello friends!
If you are in Class 12 and aiming for engineering, this one is for you.

The National Testing Agency has opened applications for **JEE Main 2027 Session 1**.
Here's everything from the official Information Bulletin, in one place.

![JEE Main 2027 Session 1 summary card](/assets/cards/in-jee-main-2027-session-1-registration-dates-summary.png)

## Session 1 dates at a glance
| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Apply online | 24 October 2026 to 22 November 2026 (up to 11:50 PM) |
| Last date to pay the fee | 22 November 2026 (up to 11:50 PM) |
| Apply with late fee | 23 November 2026 to 26 November 2026 |
| Correction window | 27 November 2026 to 29 November 2026 |
| City intimation slip | 30 December 2026 |
| Admit card | 13 January 2027 |
| Exam dates | 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027 |

The normal deadline, **22 November**, falls on a Sunday.
Payment gateways can get slow on the last night, so don't leave it till then.

## Missed the deadline? There is a late window
From 23 to 26 November you can still apply.
You pay an extra **₹500** on top of your normal fee, and it is the same for every category.

## How much is the fee?
For centres in India, the fee for one paper (B.E./B.Tech, B.Arch or B.Planning) is:

- General, male: **₹1,000**
- General, female: **₹800**
- Gen-EWS / OBC (NCL), male: **₹900**
- Gen-EWS / OBC (NCL), female: **₹800**
- SC / ST / PwD / PwBD and third gender: **₹500**

Processing charges and GST are added by the bank or payment gateway.
You can pay only online, by net banking, credit card, debit card or UPI.

## Exam timing
Each day has two shifts.
For the 3-hour paper, the first shift is **9:00 AM to 12:00 noon** and the second is **3:00 PM to 6:00 PM** (IST).

## Who can apply
There is no age limit for JEE Main 2027.
You can apply if you passed Class 12 (or equivalent) in 2025 or 2026, or are appearing in 2027.

Colleges may still have their own age rules, so it's worth checking the institute you want.

## What about Session 2?
Session 2 is in April 2027.
As per the bulletin, applications run from **5 January 2027 to 3 February 2027**, and the exams are on **2 to 7 April 2027**.

You can use the same application number, and NTA will also put out a separate notice.

## Quick tips before you fill the form
- Apply only on the official site, jeemain.nta.nic.in.
- Use a recent colour photo with a white background, and keep your face clearly visible, ears included and no mask.
- Make sure your name and date of birth match your Aadhaar and Class 10 records.
- Submit only one form. NTA says multiple forms will lead to cancellation of the result.

All the best for your preparation!
Take it one chapter at a time, you've got this.
