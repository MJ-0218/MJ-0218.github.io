Hello travellers!
Good news if you go between Varanasi and Delhi often.

**Vande Bharat Sleeper** trains are coming to the **Varanasi–New Delhi** route.
UP Chief Minister **Yogi Adityanath** and Railway Minister **Ashwini Vaishnaw** are set to flag them off from Varanasi on **31 October 2026**.

![Vande Bharat Sleeper Varanasi card](/assets/cards/in-vande-bharat-sleeper-varanasi-new-delhi-timings-stops-summary.png)

## Timings at a glance

| Direction | Departs | Arrives |
|---|---|---|
| Varanasi → New Delhi | 20:50 | 06:10 (next morning) |
| New Delhi → Varanasi | 21:40 | 07:10 (next morning) |

So you board after dinner and wake up at your destination.
That's the whole idea of an overnight sleeper: no hotel night lost to travel.

## Stops on the way
Between Varanasi and New Delhi, the train connects:

- Prayagraj
- Kanpur Central
- Aligarh
- Ghaziabad

## Which day it doesn't run
The service runs **six days a week**.

- From **Varanasi**: no service on **Fridays**
- From **New Delhi**: no service on **Saturdays**

Worth remembering before you plan a weekend trip.

## How much time do you save?
Compared with the existing **Shiv Ganga Express**, the new train is faster by:

- **55 minutes** from Varanasi to New Delhi
- **35 minutes** from New Delhi to Varanasi

## What's on board
Indian Railways lists a long set of features. The ones you'll notice most:

- **USB Type A and C charging ports**, plus a 3-pin socket at your berth
- **Hot water showers** in First AC
- **Wi-Fi router** with access point network
- **Odour-free vacuum toilets**
- **Special berths and toilets** for differently-abled passengers
- **CCTV**, emergency push buttons and a smoke/fire detection system
- **Train Collision Avoidance System (TCAS)** and fire barrier doors

There's also a spacious luggage room, which is handy for pilgrims and families with big bags.

## Where else it runs
Vande Bharat Sleeper first started on the **Howrah–Guwahati** corridor in **January 2026**.
Varanasi–New Delhi extends it to more regions.

## Before you book
Fares and booking details weren't in the announcement.
Check **IRCTC** or the official railway enquiry for current availability and fares.

Have a comfortable journey!
