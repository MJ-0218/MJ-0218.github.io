Namaste farmer friends!
The wait is over. The government has confirmed the date for the **24th instalment of PM-KISAN**.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will release it on **30 October 2026** from **Banaskantha, Gujarat**.

![PM-KISAN 24th instalment card](/assets/cards/in-pm-kisan-24th-instalment-october-30-2026-summary.png)

## The key details
- **Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Total amount:** more than **₹18,690 crore** (announced earlier as around ₹19,000 crore)
- **Farmers covered:** over **9.34 crore**
- **Place:** Banaskantha, Gujarat

The PM is scheduled to start the development programme in Banaskantha at around **10:30 AM**.
The instalment release is part of that event.

Through the first 23 instalments, more than **₹4.47 lakh crore** has already been paid to eligible farmers.

## How much will each farmer get?
PM-KISAN gives **₹6,000 a year** to small and marginal farmers.
It's paid in **three equal instalments of ₹2,000** each, directly to bank accounts through **DBT**.

So for this instalment, the amount per eligible farmer is **₹2,000**.

## What else is happening at the event
At the same Banaskantha event, the PM will also:
- launch on-ground work under **12 approved horticulture clusters**, covering about **1.46 lakh hectares**,
- dedicate **521 Agriculture Infrastructure Fund** projects worth about **₹4,950 crore**, like cold storage, warehouses and processing units.

## Didn't get the money? Check these
If the SMS doesn't arrive after 30 October, check:
1. Your **e-KYC** is complete.
2. Your **bank account is Aadhaar-seeded** and active.
3. Your name and details match your land records.

You can check your beneficiary status on the official PM-KISAN portal or ask at your nearest CSC or agriculture office.

## Beware of fake messages
Don't share OTPs or bank details with anyone calling about "PM-KISAN money."
The instalment comes straight to your bank account. You don't need to pay anyone for it.

Wishing every farmer family a happy harvest season!
