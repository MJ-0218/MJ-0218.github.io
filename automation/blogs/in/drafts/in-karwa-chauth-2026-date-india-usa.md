Hello friends!
Is Karwa Chauth on the 28th or the 29th this year? The confusion is real, so let's clear it up.

In India, **Karwa Chauth 2026 falls on Thursday, 29 October**.

![Karwa Chauth 2026 date card](/assets/cards/in-karwa-chauth-2026-date-india-usa-summary.png)

## Why some people say 28 October
The date depends on the Chaturthi tithi in your time zone.
For anyone celebrating in the **USA or Canada**, the tithi falls a day earlier, so the fast is on **Wednesday, 28 October**.

So if your family is split between India and North America, you may be fasting on different days. That's normal!

## How the day goes
- **Sargi before sunrise:** the pre-dawn meal, often sent or prepared by the mother-in-law.
- **The fast:** married women keep it from sunrise to moonrise.
- **Evening puja:** prayers to Chauth Mata with the karwa and thali.
- **Moonrise:** the fast ends after offering arghya to the moon.

## Moonrise time: check your own city
Moonrise is different in every city, and even a few minutes matter on this day.
Look up your city's moonrise in a trusted panchang the morning of the fast, rather than relying on one national time.

## Small tips for the day
- Eat a filling sargi with water-rich fruits and some protein.
- If the sky is cloudy, many families follow the panchang moonrise time; ask your elders what your family does.
- If you're unwell, pregnant or on medication, talk to your doctor before fasting.

Wishing everyone a joyful Karwa Chauth!
