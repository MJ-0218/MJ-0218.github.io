Hello friends!
Lots of you are asking whether banks will be shut tomorrow for Karva Chauth.
Short answer: **for most of India, no**.

![Karva Chauth bank holiday card](/assets/cards/in-bank-holiday-october-29-2026-karva-chauth-summary.png)

## Who gets a holiday on 29 October?
RBI's holiday list for October 2026 shows **Karva Chauth on Thursday, 29 October**.
But the holiday is marked for just **one centre: Shimla**.

So if you're in Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur, Lucknow or anywhere else on the RBI list, your branch should be open as usual.

## What about the rest of the week?
- **Friday, 30 October**: no holiday on the RBI list.
- **Saturday, 31 October**: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's Birthday, a holiday only in **Ahmedabad**.
- **Sunday, 1 November**: banks closed, as on every Sunday.

31 October is the fifth Saturday of the month.
RBI's rule closes banks only on the **second and fourth Saturdays**, so most branches will open that day.

## Planning around Karva Chauth?
If you're in Shimla, finish cheque deposits and branch work by Wednesday, 28 October.
UPI, net banking and ATMs aren't affected by branch holidays.

## How to check your own city
Open the RBI holiday page, pick **October** and **2026**, and press Go.
You'll see a grid of cities with a dot under each holiday date.

If there's no dot under 29 for your city, it's a normal working day there.
It takes less than a minute, and it saves a wasted trip.

## Why the list changes from state to state
Bank holidays in India are set centre by centre, based on the festivals each state observes.
That's why one festival can shut banks in one city and not even show up in another.

Karva Chauth is a big day across north India, yet only Shimla has it as a bank holiday this year.

Wishing everyone celebrating a very happy Karva Chauth!
May the moon rise early for you tomorrow.
