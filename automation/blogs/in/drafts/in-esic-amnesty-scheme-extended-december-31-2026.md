Hello everyone!
If your business has a pending court case with ESIC, here's some breathing room.

The **Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC)** has extended its **New Amnesty Scheme, 2025** until **31 December 2026**.

![ESIC amnesty card](/assets/cards/in-esic-amnesty-scheme-extended-december-31-2026-summary.png)

## What changed
The scheme first ran from **1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026**.
After reviewing representations from stakeholders, ESIC has added **three more months**, from **1 October 2026 to 31 December 2026**.

The terms and conditions stay the **same**.

## What the scheme is for
It's a **one-time dispute resolution** scheme.
The aim is to cut the backlog of court cases and encourage compliance under the erstwhile ESI Act.

Employers get a structured way to settle legal disputes with ESIC **out of court**.

## What you can settle
- **Ad-hoc assessments:** pay the actual contributions and interest, and **no damages** will be levied.
- **Dues already paid:** cases can be withdrawn if you pay **10% of the disputed damages**.
- **Records or late payment cases:** cases ESIC filed for non-submission of records or delayed payments, which were later complied with, will be withdrawn **with court permission**.

## Who is eligible
Court cases filed **up to 31 March 2025** are eligible.
ESIC says the extension gives eligible **employers and Insured Persons** extra time to use the scheme.

## Where to find the details
ESIC has published a flyer with the scheme details on **esic.gov.in**.
Talk to your ESIC regional office or your compliance advisor for your specific case.

## Don't miss the date
The extended window closes on **31 December 2026**.
If you think your case qualifies, it's better to start the paperwork well before the year-end rush.

All the best!
