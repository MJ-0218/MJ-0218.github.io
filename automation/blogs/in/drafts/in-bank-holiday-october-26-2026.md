Hello friends!
Heading to the bank on Monday? Check your city first.
RBI's holiday list marks **Monday, 26 October 2026** as a bank holiday in some cities, not all.

![26 October bank holiday card](/assets/cards/in-bank-holiday-october-26-2026-summary.png)

## Why 26 October?

RBI lists 26 October for **Laxmi Puja / Accession Day / Maharishi Valmiki's Birthday**.
Which one applies depends on the state.

## Cities where banks are closed

According to RBI's October 2026 holiday matrix, banks are closed on 26 October in:

- **Agartala**
- **Bengaluru**
- **Chandigarh**
- **Jammu**
- **Shimla**
- **Srinagar**

Banks in other cities, including Mumbai, New Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata and Hyderabad, are not on the list for that day.

## Other bank holidays left in October

- **Thursday, 29 October**: Karva Chauth, in **Shimla**
- **Saturday, 31 October**: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's Birthday, in **Ahmedabad**
