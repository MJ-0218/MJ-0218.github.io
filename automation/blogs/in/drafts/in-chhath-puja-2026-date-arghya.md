Hello friends!
Diwali is almost here, and right after it comes one of the most beautiful festivals of the year, **Chhath Puja**.

If you're planning travel home to Bihar, Jharkhand or eastern UP, here are the 2026 dates in one place.

## Chhath Puja 2026 at a glance
| Day | Date | Ritual |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Friday, 13 November | Nahay Khay |
| Day 2 | Saturday, 14 November | Kharna |
| Day 3 | Sunday, 15 November | Sandhya Arghya (evening offering) |
| Day 4 | Monday, 16 November | Usha Arghya (sunrise offering) |

The main Chhath day this year is **Sunday, 15 November**, when devotees offer Arghya to the setting sun.
The festival ends on **Monday, 16 November** with the offering at sunrise.

## The four days, simply explained
Chhath is spread over four days: **Nahay Khay, Kharna, Sandhya Arghya and Usha Arghya**.

- **Nahay Khay**: the vrati takes a holy bath and eats one simple, satvik meal.
- **Kharna**: a day-long fast that is opened in the evening with prasad, usually gur kheer and roti.
- **Sandhya Arghya**: families gather at a river or pond and offer Arghya to the setting sun.
- **Usha Arghya**: the offering to the rising sun, after which the long fast is broken.

It's a festival known for discipline, purity and gratitude to nature.
That's why so many people try hard to be home for it, even if it means a long train ride.

The fasting is tough.
So if someone in your family is the vrati this year, help with the cooking, the cleaning and carrying the soop and daura to the ghat.

## Is 16 November a bank holiday?
Yes, in some places.
RBI's holiday list for November 2026 shows **Chhath Puja (Prath Arghya) on 16 November**.

It's a state-wise holiday, so banks in other cities stay open.
You can check your city on the RBI holiday page before you plan any branch visit.

## Quick tips if you're travelling
- Chhath falls just a few days after Diwali (8 November), so trains and flights to the east fill up fast. Book as early as you can.
- Ghats get crowded on the evening of 15 November. Reach early, and keep an eye on kids near the water.
- Exact Arghya times depend on your city's sunrise and sunset, so check a local panchang for the minute-by-minute timing.

Regional calendars can differ by a day, so it's worth confirming with your family pandit too.
