Hello friends! Heavy rain has shut schools in parts of Tamil Nadu again today.
Here's a quick, clear list of where schools are closed on **22 October**, and what parents in Chennai should know.

![Tamil Nadu school holiday summary card](/assets/cards/in-tamil-nadu-school-holiday-oct-22-2026-summary.png)

## Schools closed on 22 October

The district administrations have declared a holiday in these four districts:

- **Tirunelveli**
- **Thoothukudi**
- **Kanniyakumari**
- **Tenkasi**

Orders can be added during the morning if the rain gets worse.
So please keep an eye on your District Collector's announcement or your school's message group.

## What about Chennai?

Schools in Chennai, Tiruvallur, Kancheepuram and Chengalpattu were closed on 21 October after heavy overnight rain.
For 22 October, no closure order has been issued for these districts so far.

Chennai got a lot of rain in this spell.
MGR Nagar recorded 15 cm, the highest in the city.

## The rain isn't over yet

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has warned of more heavy rain over Tamil Nadu in the coming days.
That means more holiday announcements are possible, often late at night or early in the morning.

## A small checklist for parents

- Check the District Collector's official social media page before leaving home.
- Avoid waterlogged roads and keep kids away from open drains and fallen wires.
- Keep a raincoat, a spare pair of socks and a charged phone in the school bag.
- For the latest alerts, look at the IMD website (mausam.imd.gov.in) or the Tamil Nadu disaster management updates.

## Quick answers

**Is it a holiday for all of Tamil Nadu?**
No. Each district decides on its own, so a holiday in one district doesn't apply to the next.

**Are colleges closed too?**
It depends on the order. Some orders cover only schools, others include colleges, so read the full announcement for your district.

**My school sent a message, but the Collector hasn't posted anything. What now?**
Follow your school's message for your child's school.
If you're unsure, a quick call to the school office is the safest way to check.

**What if the order comes after we've left home?**
It happens on rainy mornings, sadly.
If the roads look bad, it's okay to wait it out somewhere safe instead of pushing through water.

Stay dry, stay safe, and take care of the little ones today!
