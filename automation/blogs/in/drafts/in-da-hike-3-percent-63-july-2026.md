Hello friends!
Good news just in time for the festive season, especially if you or your parents work for the central government or draw a central pension.

The Union Cabinet on **Wednesday, 28 October** approved a **3 percentage point hike** in Dearness Allowance (DA) and Dearness Relief (DR).

![DA hike card](/assets/cards/in-da-hike-3-percent-63-july-2026-summary.png)

## The key numbers
- **New rate:** DA and DR go from **60% to 63%** of basic pay and pension.
- **Effective from:** **July 1, 2026**.
- **Who benefits:** about **48.54 lakh** central government employees and **68.71 lakh** pensioners.
- **Cost:** about **₹10,016.10 crore** a year for DA and DR together.

The hike follows the formula based on the **7th Central Pay Commission** recommendations.

## How much more will you get?
It's simple: take your **basic pay** (or basic pension) and multiply by 3%.

| Basic pay / pension | Extra per month (3%) | New DA at 63% |
|---|---|---|
| ₹20,000 | ₹600 | ₹12,600 |
| ₹35,000 | ₹1,050 | ₹22,050 |
| ₹50,000 | ₹1,500 | ₹31,500 |
| ₹80,000 | ₹2,400 | ₹50,400 |

These are illustrations only.
Your exact amount depends on your own basic pay or pension.

## The second hike of 2026
DA is a cost-of-living adjustment for employees, and DR is the same benefit for pensioners.
Both are meant to compensate for rising prices.

Earlier this year, in **April 2026**, the Cabinet approved a **2% instalment from January 1, 2026**, taking the rate from 58% to 60%.
Today's decision is the instalment **from July 1, 2026**, which takes it to 63%.

## What should you do now?
Nothing to apply for, it's automatic.
Since the hike counts from July 1, keep an eye on your salary slip or pension statement over the next few months, and check your department's notice for when the revised amount is credited.

Wishing all our central government families a very happy Diwali!
