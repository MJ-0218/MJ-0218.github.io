Hello friends!
If you or your parents draw a pension, the yearly life certificate is a task you can't skip.

The good news: you don't always have to stand in a bank queue for it.
India Post can help you do it at the post office, or even at your doorstep.

![Life certificate card](/assets/cards/in-life-certificate-pensioners-india-post-doorstep-70-summary.png)

## What's happening
The **Department of Posts** and **India Post Payments Bank (IPPB)** help pensioners submit **Digital Life Certificates (DLC)** through the **Jeevan Pramaan** service.
It's part of the nationwide **Digital Life Certificate 5.0 Campaign**.

In Goa, for example, the post office service starts from **November 1, 2026**.
It covers Central Government, Goa State Government and **EPFO** pensioners.

## How the digital certificate works
It uses **Aadhaar-based biometric authentication**, with either your **fingerprint** or **face**.

Once verification succeeds:
- the certificate is generated digitally,
- you get an **SMS confirmation**, and
- it goes to your **Pension Disbursing Authority**.

## Two ways to do it
**1. At the post office.**
Visit your nearest post office and complete the process there.

**2. At home.**
If travelling is hard, a postman or Gramin Dak Sevak can come to you.
This helps pensioners in remote areas and anyone who finds bank visits tiring.

## What the doorstep service costs
In Goa, the doorstep service costs **₹70, including GST**, for each certificate that's generated successfully.
There's **no extra delivery charge** on top.

## How to book (Goa example)
- **Post Info app:** register a request in the official app.
- **Phone IPPB:** North Goa **9218302131**, South Goa **9218302130**.
- **Post office:** visit or call your local branch.

Outside Goa, ask your nearest post office about the same service in your area.

## Keep these ready
- Aadhaar number
- Registered mobile number
- **PPO number** (Pension Payment Order) or Pension ID
- Bank account details

## Quick tips
- Make sure the mobile number linked to Aadhaar is working, since the confirmation comes by SMS.
- Keep a screenshot or note of the SMS once you receive it.

Wishing all our elders a smooth, queue-free November.
Take care!
