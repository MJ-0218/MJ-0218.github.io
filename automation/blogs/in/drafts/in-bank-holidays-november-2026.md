Hello friends!
November is a busy month, with Diwali, Chhath and Guru Nanak Jayanti all coming up.
If you've got a cheque to deposit or a locker visit planned, it's worth checking the bank calendar first.

Here's the official list from the Reserve Bank of India's holiday matrix for November 2026.

![November 2026 bank holidays card](/assets/cards/in-bank-holidays-november-2026-summary.png)

## First: holidays are different in each state

RBI publishes holidays city by city.
So a date on this list doesn't mean every bank in India is closed that day. It depends on where your branch is.

Open the RBI holiday page, pick your city and November, and you'll see your exact days.

## November 2026 bank holidays (RBI list)

![November 2026 at a glance](/assets/cards/in-bank-holidays-november-2026-days.png)

- **Monday, 9 November**: Diwali / Laxmi Puja (Deepawali) / Govardhan Pooja / Vishwakarma Day
- **Tuesday, 10 November**: Diwali (Bali Pratipada) / Govardhan Puja / Vikram Samvat New Year Day
- **Wednesday, 11 November**: Laxmi Puja / Ningol Chakkouba / Bhratridwitiya / Bhai Dooj / Chitragupt Jayanti
- **Friday, 13 November**: Wangala Festival
- **Monday, 16 November**: Chhath Puja (Prath Arghya)
- **Friday, 20 November**: Igas-Bagwal
- **Monday, 23 November**: Seng Kut Snem
- **Tuesday, 24 November**: Guru Nanak Jayanti / Kartika Purnima / Rahas Purnima
- **Friday, 27 November**: Kanakadasa Jayanthi

Diwali itself falls on Sunday, 8 November this year, so banks are already shut that day.
In many states, the next working days carry the festival holidays.

## Don't forget the Saturdays

RBI notes that all banks observe a holiday on the **second and fourth Saturdays**.
In November 2026, that's **Saturday, 14 November** and **Saturday, 28 November**.

Add the Sundays (1, 8, 15, 22 and 29 November) and some cities will have long breaks around Diwali.

## Quick checklist before Diwali week

- Withdraw some cash before the weekend of 7–8 November.
- Deposit cheques by Friday, 6 November if they're time-sensitive.
- Check your city's list on the RBI page; it was last updated on 19 October 2026.

Happy festive season, and plan your bank work early!
