Hello friends!
If you deliver food, drive a cab through an app or do any kind of platform work, this one is about you.

The **Ministry of Labour & Employment** has notified the constitution of the **National Social Security Board for Unorganised Workers and Gig & Platform Workers**.
The announcement came on **30 October 2026**.

![Gig workers board card](/assets/cards/in-gig-platform-workers-national-social-security-board-notified-summary.png)

## What exactly was notified
This is a **board**, set up under the **Code on Social Security**.
It is not a new payment or a new registration drive by itself.

Its job is to help the government design and track welfare schemes for gig, platform and unorganised workers.

The Ministry calls it a historic shift.
Why? Because it brings **digital gig and platform workers** into India's formal welfare system, right alongside traditional unorganised workers.

## Who sits on the board
The board is chaired by the **Union Minister for Labour & Employment**.
Members include representatives from:

- Central and State Governments
- Aggregator companies (the apps)
- Employers
- Civil society organisations
- Workers' unions
- Gig & platform worker associations

So for the first time, the apps and the workers' groups sit at the same table.

## How often it meets
The board has to meet **at least thrice a year**.
Its meeting rules will follow what the Central Government prescribes.

## What the board will do
Here's the list of functions, in plain words:

- **Recommend schemes** for different groups of unorganised, gig and platform workers
- **Advise** the Centre on issues referred to it under the Code
- **Monitor** welfare schemes the Centre runs for these workers
- **Review record keeping** done at the State level
- **Review spending** from the fund and account
- Take up **any other work** the Centre assigns

## Quick answers

**Is this the same as e-Shram?**
No, this release is only about the board.
It doesn't announce any change to registration.

**Will the apps have a say?**
Yes.
Aggregator companies are named among the members, and so are workers' unions and gig associations.

**Who decides the final schemes?**
The board recommends and advises.
The Central Government frames the schemes.

## What this means for you right now
There's no new benefit amount announced in this release.
Think of it as the body that will shape and check those schemes going forward.

Keep an eye on official updates from the Labour Ministry.
When a scheme comes out of these meetings, we'll break it down here too.

Stay safe on the roads and take care!
