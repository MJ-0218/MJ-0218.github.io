If you earn a little above ₹15,000 a month and don't have PF, this one's for you.
The wage ceiling for **mandatory EPFO coverage** has been raised to **₹25,000 per month**.

![EPFO wage ceiling card](/assets/cards/in-epfo-wage-ceiling-25000-withdrawal-rules-explained-summary.png)

## What changed
The ceiling was last revised in **September 2014**, from ₹6,500 to **₹15,000**.
In **September 2026**, an increase to **₹25,000 per month** was approved.

The government estimates this will bring **over 51 lakh more employees** under mandatory coverage.
Coverage still depends on the applicable statutory provisions, so check with your employer or HR for how it applies to you.

## What EPFO gives you
EPFO's social security rests on three things:
- **Provident fund** savings with interest
- **Pension** on retirement, superannuation or disability
- **Insurance** protection

## Withdrawing from PF: the simplified rules
EPFO has merged **13 partial withdrawal provisions into three categories**:
- **Essential Needs:** illness, education, marriage
- **Housing Needs**
- **Special Circumstances**

A few limits worth knowing:
- Up to **10 withdrawals for education** and **5 for marriage**. Earlier, both shared a combined limit of 3.
- Eligible members can withdraw **up to 75% of their PF balance**, including employee and employer shares, subject to conditions.
- The minimum service requirement is now a standard **12 months**.

## Faster claims, less paperwork
- The **auto-settlement limit** for eligible advance claims is now **₹5 lakh**, so these can be processed without manual steps.
- For eligible online claims, you **no longer need to upload a cheque-leaf image or attested passbook**.

## For employers: two schemes to note
- **VISHWAS, 2026:** one-time settlement of eligible disputes over damages or penalties, applied for online on the EPFO Employer Portal.
- **AMNESTY, 2026:** helps establishments regularise PF trusts. It's **open until 28 December 2026**.

## Quick to-do
- Check your **UAN** is active and your bank details are updated.
- Ask HR whether the new ceiling changes your PF status.

Stay informed and plan well!
