Hello friends! If you live on the east coast, please keep an eye on the weather this week.
A storm over the Bay of Bengal, **Cyclone Arnab**, is heading for the Andhra Pradesh coast.

Here is what we know so far, kept short and simple.

![Cyclone Arnab summary card](/assets/cards/in-cyclone-arnab-andhra-odisha-landfall-oct-28-summary.png)

## Where and when it will hit
IMD expects the storm to cross the Andhra Pradesh coast **between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam**.
Landfall is likely by the **forenoon of 28 October** (Wednesday).

The system was forecast to keep strengthening, up to a **very severe cyclonic storm** by Wednesday morning.

## How strong the winds may be
At landfall, IMD forecasts sustained winds of **115 to 125 kmph**, with gusts up to **140 kmph**.
That is strong enough to bring down hoardings and weak structures, so stay indoors.

## Odisha: schools shut, red alert in the south
Schools, colleges and Anganwadi centres will stay closed on **Wednesday and Thursday (28–29 October)** in five districts:

- Ganjam
- Gajapati
- Rayagada
- Koraput
- Malkangiri

The state has kept **302 rescue teams** ready and asked people in low-lying areas to move to shelters.

## Andhra Pradesh: officials on high alert
Andhra Pradesh Home Minister V. Anitha asked officials in affected districts to stay on high alert.
NDRF and SDRF teams are being kept ready, and dangerous hoardings are to be removed.

People in coastal areas may be shifted to safer places if needed.

## Simple steps for your family
- Follow your district administration's updates and evacuation advice.
- Keep phones charged and some drinking water and food at home.
- Stay away from the beach, and don't go out to sea.
- Tie down or bring in loose items from balconies and rooftops.

Please stay safe, and check on elderly neighbours too. Take care, everyone!
