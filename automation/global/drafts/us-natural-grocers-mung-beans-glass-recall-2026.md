If you buy bulk beans at Natural Grocers, check your pantry today.
The company is recalling its **Organic 2-Pound Mung Beans** because the bags may contain pieces of glass.

Here's what to look for and what to do.

![Mung beans recall card](/assets/cards/us-natural-grocers-mung-beans-glass-recall-2026-summary.png)

## What's recalled

The product is **Natural Grocers Brand Bulk Organic Mung Beans** in **2-pound clear plastic bags** with **UPC 000080112600**.

Only these pack dates are included. You'll find the date in the lower left corner of the label:

- 26-160
- 26-173
- 26-196
- 26-224
- 26-258

These lots began shipping on **June 10, 2026**.

## Why it was recalled

The recall started after customers complained about sharp glass fragments in the product.
Swallowing glass can damage teeth, cut the mouth and throat, or perforate the intestine, the notice warns.

No illnesses or injuries have been reported so far.

## Where it was sold

The beans may have shipped to Natural Grocers stores in **22 states**:
AR, AZ, CO, IA, ID, KS, LA, MN, MO, MT, ND, NE, NM, NV, OK, OR, SD, TX, UT, WA, WI and WY.

## What to do

Stop using the beans right away.
Throw them out, or take them back to the store for a full refund or credit.

Questions go to the company at **303-226-1434**, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Mountain Time.

Check that label before your next batch of mung bean soup!
