Quick answer first: **Black Friday 2026 is Friday, Nov. 27.**
That's the day right after Thanksgiving, which lands on **Thursday, Nov. 26** this year.

If you're mapping out deals, travel or time off, here's the whole week in one place.

![Black Friday 2026 card](/assets/cards/us-when-is-black-friday-2026-date-market-hours-summary.png)

## The Thanksgiving week calendar

| Day | Date | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving | Thu, Nov. 26 | Federal holiday |
| Black Friday | Fri, Nov. 27 | Not a federal holiday |
| Cyber Monday | Mon, Nov. 30 | The Monday after Thanksgiving |

Black Friday always follows Thanksgiving, so its date moves every year.
In 2026 it comes on the 27th.

## Is Black Friday a federal holiday?
No.
The federal government's official holiday list for 2026 includes **Thanksgiving Day on Nov. 26**, but not the Friday after it.

That means federal offices observe Thursday as the holiday.
Whether you get Friday off depends on your employer, not a federal rule.

## Is the stock market open on Black Friday?
Yes, but only for part of the day.

The **New York Stock Exchange is closed on Thanksgiving**, Thursday, Nov. 26.
On **Friday, Nov. 27**, NYSE markets **close early at 1:00 p.m. Eastern** (1:15 p.m. for eligible options).

So if you trade, plan any Friday moves for the morning.

## Are banks open?
The **Federal Reserve** observes Thanksgiving on **Nov. 26**, so expect bank transfers and deposits to pause that Thursday.

The Fed's holiday list doesn't include the day after Thanksgiving.
Individual bank branches set their own Friday hours, so check yours if you need to go in person.

## Planning tips for the week
- **Payments:** if a bill is due on Thanksgiving, pay it a day or two early so a bank holiday doesn't slow it down.
- **Price tracking:** jot down today's prices on the items you want, so you can tell a real deal from a marked-up "sale."
- **Budget:** decide your list and limit before the weekend, and stick to it.

## Looking ahead to 2027
NYSE's calendar already shows the day after Thanksgiving in 2027 falls on **Friday, Nov. 26, 2027**.
That's next year's Black Friday, if you like to plan way ahead.

Happy shopping, and have a great Thanksgiving!
