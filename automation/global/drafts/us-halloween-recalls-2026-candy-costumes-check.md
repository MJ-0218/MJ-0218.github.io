Before the kids dump their candy haul on the table tonight, take two minutes for this.
Several Halloween items have been recalled this year, and some may already be sitting in your closet from last season.

Here are four from the **Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)**, with what to look for and how to get your money back.

![Halloween recall card](/assets/cards/us-halloween-recalls-2026-candy-costumes-check-summary.png)

## 1. Ricky Joy Sour Crush Rolling Liquid Candy
- **Problem:** the rolling ball on top can detach, a **choking hazard**.
- **How many:** about **2.3 million** bottles.
- **Look for:** 1.85 fl oz bottles with the Ricky Joy logo, "EXTREME SOUR," "SOUR CRUSH," and a green monster. Flavors: blue raspberry, strawberry, green apple and watermelon.
- **Sold:** H-E-B, Forman Mill, candy stores and online, including Walmart.com, from January 2023 through May 2026.
- **What to do:** stop using it, keep it away from kids, and contact Ricky Joy for a refund at **404-975-0352**. They'll send a prepaid return label.

## 2. CIYTI Light-Up Princess Costume
- **Problem:** the crown, scepter and remote have **button batteries** kids can reach easily. Swallowed batteries can cause internal chemical burns and death.
- **How many:** about **94**.
- **Sold:** Amazon, February to June 2025, for $21 to $35.
- **What to do:** take it from children, write **"RECALLED"** on each piece with permanent marker, throw it away, and email a photo to **Norakid2026@outlook.com** for a full refund.

## 3. Gavoyeat Halloween Light-Up Rings (50-pack)
- **Problem:** button batteries in the rings are **easy for kids to access**.
- **Look for:** model **43398-68657**, "B07GF8VD7N" on the back, and "Holiday Party Favors LED Light Up Ring."
- **Sold:** Amazon, June 2025 through January 2026, about $15.
- **What to do:** remove and properly dispose of the batteries, toss the rings, and email a photo to **recalledgavoyeat@yeah.net**.

## 4. Besslly Halloween Pumpkin Carving Kit
- **Problem:** the tea lights hold **15 button batteries** that kids can get to, and the kit lacks the warnings required under Reese's Law.
- **Look for:** model **CHD-014-2**.
- **Sold:** Amazon, March 2024 through December 2025, for $9 to $20.
- **What to do:** stop using it and email a disposal photo to **tingjmng@outlook.com** for a full refund.

## One rule for all button batteries
CPSC notes button cell batteries are hazardous waste.
Dispose of or recycle them by following your **local hazardous waste procedures**, not the kitchen trash.

Have a safe and happy Halloween!
