Got a letter about a $500 health insurance refund, or heard a neighbor got one?
Here's who's eligible for the new ACA refund checks, based on the White House fact sheet.

![ACA refund checks card](/assets/cards/us-aca-500-refund-checks-who-qualifies-2026-summary.png)

## The basics
The White House announced on **September 10, 2026** that it will send **$500 per person** to **nearly 1 million Americans** who buy coverage through the federal ACA marketplace.
Checks are being sent **beginning in October 2026**.

## Who qualifies
According to the fact sheet, the refunds go to people who:

- bought a plan through the marketplace in a state that uses the **federal exchange** (HealthCare.gov), and
- **did not receive premium assistance**, meaning they paid the full premium themselves.

It's **$500 per person**, so a household with more than one eligible member may see more than one check.

## The 30 states
Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, Delaware, Florida, Hawaii, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Louisiana, Michigan, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, West Virginia, Wisconsin and Wyoming.

If your state runs its own exchange (for example, California or New York), you're not on this list.

## Where does the money come from?
The White House says the refunds come from surplus **exchange "user fees"** that are built into marketplace premiums.
You don't need to understand the funding to cash the check, but it explains why only federal-exchange states are included.

## Watch out for scams
Any time government money is in the news, fake "refund" calls and texts follow.
Don't share your Social Security number or bank details with anyone who contacts you out of the blue about this check.

If you're unsure, go straight to **HealthCare.gov** or call the number on your official marketplace notice.

## Shopping for 2027 coverage?
Open enrollment for 2027 marketplace plans starts **Nov. 1, 2026** and runs to **Jan. 15, 2027**.
Sign up by **Dec. 15** if you want coverage to start on Jan. 1.

That's a good moment to compare plans and check whether you now qualify for premium help.
