Missed it? You're not out of luck. California's deadline to register online or by mail for the **Nov. 3, 2026** General Election was Monday, Oct. 19. But the state has a safety net called Same Day Voter Registration, and it lets you register and vote in person.

![Missed the deadline summary card](/assets/cards/us-california-same-day-voter-registration-2026-summary.png)

## What Same Day Voter Registration is

State law calls it Conditional Voter Registration. The Secretary of State describes it as a safety net for Californians who miss the deadline to register, or who need to update their registration, before an election.

It covers the last 14 days before an election. If you need to register or re-register in that window, you can do it and vote at the same visit.

## Where you can do it

You have three options, all in person.

| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| County elections office | Register and vote in person |
| Vote center | Find early voting locations at caearlyvoting.sos.ca.gov |
| Polling place | On Election Day, use the state's polling place lookup tool |

![Where you can register in person](/assets/cards/us-california-same-day-voter-registration-2026-steps.png)

Not sure which locations near you offer it? Your county elections office can tell you.

## How your ballot gets counted

Here's the part people ask about most. Your ballot is set aside and counted once the county elections office finishes verifying your registration. So it may be counted a little later than a regular ballot, but it does count once you're verified.

## Who this is for

A few common cases:

- You just turned old enough to vote and never registered.
- You moved to a new county and didn't update your address in time.
- You changed your name and your registration still shows the old one.
- You simply forgot. It happens.

In each case, the fix is the same: go in person, register, and vote at that visit.

## Military, overseas and voters with disabilities

If you're a military or overseas voter, or a voter with a disability, contact your county elections office. They'll walk you through the conditional registration and voting options available to you.

## Before you go

First, see if you're already registered. Plenty of people assume they need to re-register when they don't. You can check your status on the Secretary of State's website in a couple of minutes.

If you moved or changed your name since you last voted, that's when updating matters. Same Day Registration handles that too.

Make a plan to vote, and take care!
