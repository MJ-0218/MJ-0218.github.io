If your kids brush with a Barbie, Hot Wheels or ice cream-flavored toothpaste, check the tube. About **4.3 million tubes** of Brush Buddies, evo and ARU toothpaste are under a nationwide FDA recall.

![Toothpaste recall summary](/assets/cards/us-brush-buddies-toothpaste-recall-2026-summary.png)

## What's being recalled

The FDA lists 17 products. Most are Brush Buddies children's toothpastes:

- **Character tubes:** Barbie, Hot Wheels, Crayola Silly Scents and My Little Pony
- **Ice cream flavors:** Vanilla, Mint Chocolate Chip, Cookies 'n Cream and Chocolate
- **evo:** Advanced Whitening and Sensitive
- **ARU:** Gum Health and Cavity Protection

![Products to check](/assets/cards/us-brush-buddies-toothpaste-recall-2026-products.png)

Several Brush Buddies whitening and cavity-protection tubes are on the list too. So it isn't only the kids' flavors: adults using a value-pack whitening tube from these brands should check as well.

The exact count depends on who you ask. WGAL tallies 4,301,700 tubes, while NTD reports 4,298,900. Either way, it's roughly 4.3 million.

## Why it was recalled

The stated reason is "current good manufacturing practice deviations," often shortened to cGMP. That means the products weren't made under the quality controls the FDA requires.

News reports so far don't spell out a specific defect, such as contamination. The recall is framed around how the toothpaste was made, not a confirmed problem found in a tube.

Pritam International Private Limited started the recall on Sept. 10. Ashtel Studios of Ontario, California, distributed the products. Later, the FDA classified it as a Class II recall.

## How serious is a Class II recall?

The FDA rated this a Class II recall. That's the middle tier. It means using the product may cause temporary or medically reversible health problems, while the chance of serious harm is considered remote.

So this isn't a "rush to the ER" situation. If your child has been using one of these tubes and seems fine, there's no reason to panic. Still, it's a product your kids put in their mouths twice a day.

## What to do if you have one

1. **Check the brand and flavor** against the list above.
2. **Compare the lot and expiration date** on the tube or box with the FDA Enforcement Report.
3. **If it matches, stop using it** and keep it away from kids.
4. **Contact the store or online seller** where you bought it about a return.
5. **Pick up a replacement** from a different brand in the meantime. Kids' fluoride toothpaste is easy to find.

If you aren't sure whether your tube is on the list, the FDA Enforcement Report has the full product names and lot details.

## The bottom line

About 4.3 million tubes of Brush Buddies, evo and ARU toothpaste are recalled over manufacturing problems. The risk is rated low to moderate, but swapping out a kids' toothpaste is easy. Take thirty seconds tonight to check the bathroom drawer.
