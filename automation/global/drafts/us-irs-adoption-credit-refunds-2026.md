If you adopted a child and carried part of the adoption tax credit forward from an earlier year, there may be money headed your way, and you don't have to do anything to get it. The IRS says it has **already started sending refunds to more than 50,000 taxpayers** and expects to finish for most eligible people by the end of the year.

![Automatic adoption credit refunds card](/assets/cards/us-irs-adoption-credit-refunds-2026-summary.png)

## What changed

The Treasury Department changed how it reads the adoption credit rules under the Working Families Tax Cuts. Here's the short version: for tax year 2025 only, adoption credit carryforwards from 2020–2024 can increase the part of the credit that's refundable in 2025. The limit is $5,000 per qualifying child.

## Do you need to file an amended return?

No. That's the best part. The IRS is adjusting accounts on its own and sending any refund that results. It says taxpayers shouldn't need to file an amended return to get the extra credit.

## When you'll hear from the IRS

Refunds and adjustments began in late September and will continue until they're done. If you filed your 2025 return before April 15, 2026, you'll generally get any adjustment automatically.

Some accounts take longer. If you filed under an extension, or your account has an identity theft flag, a freeze, a dependency conflict or another issue, the IRS will review it later in 2026, after the return is processed or the problem is resolved.

Either way, expect a notice in the mail, generally within 30 days of the adjustment. It'll tell you the amount and whether it became a refund or went toward another tax or non-tax debt you owe.

## What to do with that notice

The IRS asks you to:

1. Read it carefully.
2. Compare it with your 2025 tax return.
3. Keep it with your tax records.

If you'd rather not wait for the mail, an IRS Individual Online Account lets you check refund status and view notices. Details on the credit itself are at IRS.gov/AdoptionCredit.

A small but welcome surprise for adoptive families. Keep an eye on the mailbox, and take care!
