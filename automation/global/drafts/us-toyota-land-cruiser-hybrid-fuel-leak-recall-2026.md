Own a newer Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid? There's a recall you'll want to check.
Toyota is recalling certain **2024–2027 Land Cruiser Hybrid** SUVs over a possible fuel leak.

Here's what the official NHTSA record says.

![Land Cruiser Hybrid recall card](/assets/cards/us-toyota-land-cruiser-hybrid-fuel-leak-recall-2026-summary.png)

## What's wrong

The **fuel tube connection near the fuel tank may disconnect and leak fuel**.
A fuel leak near an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

The NHTSA campaign number is **26V673000**. Toyota's own recall codes are **26TB23 and 26TA23**.

## The fix

Dealers will inspect the connection and add a supplemental clamp.
If needed, they'll replace the resin fuel tube and install the clamp.

The repair is **free of charge**.

## When will owners hear from Toyota?

Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed **Nov. 29, 2026**.
You don't have to wait for the letter to check your vehicle, though.

## How to check your SUV

- Enter your VIN at **nhtsa.gov/recalls**.
- Or call Toyota customer service at **1-800-331-4331**.

If you smell fuel around your vehicle, don't ignore it. Contact your dealer right away.

Check that VIN today, and drive safe!
