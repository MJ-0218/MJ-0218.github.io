Got an electric pressure cooker that says **Power Quick Pot** on the front? Flip it around and check the label.
The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission is telling owners of one model to **stop using it right away**.

This isn't a regular recall, which makes it a little different.
There's no refund or repair on offer, so it's on you to check.

![Power Quick Pot warning card](/assets/cards/us-tristar-power-quick-pot-pressure-cooker-warning-2026-summary.png)

## Which pressure cooker
The warning covers the **6-quart Power Quick Pot** electric pressure cooker, **model Y6D-36**.
About **393,000** of them were sold.

It's stainless steel with a black pressure lid, button controls and a digital display.
"Power Quick Pot" is printed on the front, and the model number is on a label on the back.

## What's wrong with it
CPSC says the lid can open while the pot is still pressurized.
When that happens, hot food and liquid can shoot out without warning.

The agency has 24 reports of this happening, all ending in serious burns.

## Where it was sold
Walmart.com, Amazon.com, eBay.com and other retailers nationwide sold it from about **July 2018 to November 2023**.
It cost between $44 and $160.

## Why there's no recall
The importer, Tristar Products of Altamonte Springs, Florida, **refused** to recall the cookers or offer a fix, according to CPSC.
That's why the agency put out a public warning instead.

## What to do
- Stop using it now.
- Throw it away.
- Don't sell it or give it to someone else, even at a yard sale.

If you've had a problem with one, you can report it to CPSC at SaferProducts.gov.

Five seconds to check the back of your cooker could save someone a nasty burn. Stay safe out there.
