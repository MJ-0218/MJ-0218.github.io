Just pulled your space heater out for the cold weather? Don't plug it in until you've checked the model number.
DREO is recalling about **338,800** of its **DR-HSH015** space heaters because they can overheat and catch fire.

![DREO heater recall card](/assets/cards/us-dreo-space-heater-recall-dr-hsh015-2026-summary.png)

## Which heaters are recalled
- **Brand and model:** DREO space heater, model **DR-HSH015**
- **What it looks like:** a black ceramic electric heater, about **11.5 inches wide** and **13 inches tall**, with a remote control
- **Made:** only units manufactured from **April 2025 through January 2026**

The manufacture date (in YYYY/MM format) and the model number are on a label on the **underside** of the heater.
"DREO" is printed on the front.

About **36,300** more were sold in Canada.

## The problem
It's a fire risk. CPSC says the heaters can **overheat and ignite**, posing fire and burn hazards.

DREO has received **17 reports** of heaters igniting.
Three caused property damage, and one led to a burn injury to the hand.

## Where they were sold
At **Costco** stores and online at Dreo.com, BestBuy.com, Kohls.com, Lowes.com, QVC.com, HomeDepot.com, B2BRenew.com and Costco.com.
They sold from **September 2025 through October 2026** for about **$70 to $110**.

## What to do
If yours matches, here's what CPSC says to do:

1. **Stop using** the heater right away.
2. Contact DREO for a **free replacement** heater.
3. DREO will ask you to unplug it, **cut the electrical cord** to destroy it, and send a photo of the destroyed heater.

## How to contact DREO
- Toll-free **833-820-0887**, 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET, Monday through Friday
- Email **spaceheater@realtimeresults.net**
- Online at **dreo.com** (click "PRODUCT RECALLS" at the top) or **recallrtr.com/spaceheater**

If you're not sure, flip the heater over and check the label. It only takes a minute.

Stay warm and safe this winter!
