Parents and caregivers, please check your pantry and diaper bag.

The **FDA** is warning families **not to buy or serve** Once Upon a Farm **Tractor Wheels Organic Toddler Soft-Baked Bars** because of a potentially fatal **choking hazard**.
The advice covers **every flavor, size, lot code and expiration date**.

![Tractor Wheels warning card](/assets/cards/us-once-upon-a-farm-tractor-wheels-choking-fda-warning-summary.png)

## What happened
The FDA has received several reports of toddlers **ages 1.5 to 2.5** suffering **complete airway obstruction**, unable to breathe, after eating the bars.

According to the FDA, the bar can form a **dry, sticky mass** when chewed.
Standard choking help, including **back blows and the Heimlich maneuver**, may fail to dislodge it.

Some incidents needed emergency responders and hospital observation.

## What to do right now
- **Don't serve** the bars, and **throw them away**.
- This applies to **all flavors and varieties**, whatever the lot code or date.
- If someone is choking and first aid isn't working, **call 911**.
- If your child has symptoms after eating one, contact your **health care provider**.

## Where it was sold
Nationwide online and in stores, including **Costco, Target and Walmart**.
It may also have been sold on **Amazon.com** and by other third-party sellers.

## Is there a recall?
The FDA says it told the company about the reports, and the company **agreed to start a recall**.
The FDA will update its advisory as more information comes in.

## Retailers
Retailers have been told they **should not sell** this product.

## Reporting a problem
You can report a complaint or adverse event through the FDA's **Industry and Consumer Assistance** page.

Stay safe, and please share this with other parents.
