Have a Sienna Hybrid in the driveway?
This one's worth five minutes of your time, especially if kids ride in the middle row.

Toyota is recalling **228,367** Sienna Hybrid minivans from the **2022 to 2026** model years.
It only covers the **7-passenger** versions, the ones with two separate second-row seats.

![Toyota Sienna Hybrid recall card](/assets/cards/us-toyota-sienna-hybrid-seat-back-recall-2026-summary.png)

## What's wrong
In a crash, the **second-row seat backs may fail to stay secured**.
A seat back that doesn't hold in place can't protect the person sitting there as it should, and that raises the risk of injury.

You won't notice anything in everyday driving.
Your seats will recline and lock just like they always have.

This is about how the seat behaves in the split second of a collision.
So don't wait for a warning sign or a strange noise before you check.

## What Toyota will do
Dealers will **update the software** in the supplemental restraint system (SRS) control unit, the computer that manages airbags and seatbelt pretensioners.

Minivans with the **"Mobility as a Service" (MaaS)** package get a different fix: dealers will replace both second-row seat backs.
Either way, the repair is **free**.

## Who should pay extra attention
If you regularly carry kids, grandparents or carpool passengers in the middle row, put this near the top of your to-do list.
In a lot of minivans, that's where the car seats and boosters go.

A quick dealer visit now beats wondering about it on your next road trip.

## When you'll hear from Toyota
Toyota expects to mail owner letters on **December 7, 2026**.
You don't have to wait for the letter to check whether your van is included.

## How to check your minivan
1. Find your 17-character VIN on the lower driver's side of the windshield or on your registration card.
2. Enter it at **nhtsa.gov/recalls**, or call Toyota customer service at **1-800-331-4331**.
3. Toyota's own codes for this recall are **26TB24** and **26TA24**, handy when you call a dealer.

## Quick answers
**Is the 8-passenger Sienna included?**
This recall lists only 7-passenger Sienna Hybrid models.

**Should I stop using the second row?**
NHTSA hasn't issued a "park it" or "don't ride" warning for this recall.
Still, it's smart to book the fix as soon as your dealer can do it.

Short version: check the VIN, book the free fix, and you're done.
Stay safe out there, and give your fellow Sienna owners a heads-up!
