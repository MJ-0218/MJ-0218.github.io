Veterans Day 2026 is **Wednesday, Nov. 11**. Because it lands midweek, it's easy to forget until the day you try to send a wire transfer or visit a federal office. Here's what the official schedules say.

![Veterans Day 2026 summary](/assets/cards/us-veterans-day-2026-summary.png)

## Is Veterans Day a federal holiday?

Yes. The Office of Personnel Management lists Veterans Day on Wednesday, Nov. 11 in its 2026 federal holiday schedule. That schedule is the one that sets days off for federal employees.

Since Nov. 11 falls on a Wednesday this year, there's no Friday or Monday shift to think about.

## Are banks closed?

The Federal Reserve is. Its holiday calendar lists Veterans Day on Nov. 11, 2026.

Your own bank is a separate question. Banks and credit unions set their own branch hours, so check your bank's website or app before you plan a branch visit or a time-sensitive payment.

## Is the stock market open?

Yes, based on the NYSE's published calendar. Veterans Day isn't on the NYSE's 2026 holiday list, so you can expect normal trading on Nov. 11.

## Why the date doesn't move

Unlike Memorial Day or Labor Day, Veterans Day isn't tied to a Monday. It stays on Nov. 11 every year, which is why it can land on any day of the week.

When it falls on a weekend, schedules shift. The Federal Reserve, for example, says that for holidays falling on a Saturday, Reserve Banks open the Friday before, and for holidays falling on a Sunday, all Federal Reserve offices close the following Monday. None of that applies this year, since Nov. 11 is a Wednesday.

## What about the next holiday?

Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26. That one is on all three lists we checked: OPM, the Federal Reserve and the NYSE.

The NYSE is also closing early the day after, Friday, Nov. 27, at 1 p.m. Eastern. If you trade, put that on your calendar now.

## Quick checklist for Nov. 11

- Planning a bank transfer that must land by a certain date? Send it a day early.
- Visiting a federal office? Pick another day.
- Trading stocks? Markets follow their normal schedule.
- Anything local, like schools, trash pickup or city offices, depends on your state and city. Check their sites.

## The bottom line

Veterans Day is Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026. Federal offices and the Federal Reserve take the day off, while the stock market stays open. For banks, schools and local services, check directly, because they set their own schedules.
