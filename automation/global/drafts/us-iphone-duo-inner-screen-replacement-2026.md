Just got Apple's new foldable? Take a close look at the inside screen.
Apple has confirmed that some **iPhone Duo** units have a problem with the **inner display**, only days after the phone went on sale.

If yours is one of them, Apple says you can get it replaced.

![iPhone Duo inner display card](/assets/cards/us-iphone-duo-inner-screen-replacement-2026-summary.png)

## What Apple said
In a statement reported by The Guardian and the BBC, Apple said it is aware of "an issue impacting the inner display on a small number of iPhone Duo units."
Owners who see a problem should **contact AppleCare for a replacement**.

## What owners are seeing
Photos shared online show **lines across the inner screen**, and some owners have posted **cracks along the center crease**.
The Duo is Apple's first foldable phone, so it's the first time many people are dealing with a folding display.

## How big is the problem?
We don't know yet.
Apple didn't say how many phones are affected, and experts told The Guardian there were too few examples to tell whether there's a wider fault.

So if your Duo looks fine, there's no need to panic. Just keep an eye on it for the first few weeks.

## What to do if your screen looks wrong
- **Take clear photos or a short video** of the lines or crack, with the date.
- **Contact AppleCare** through the Apple Support app, by phone, or at an Apple Store, and mention Apple's statement about the inner display.
- **Have your proof of purchase ready**, along with the serial number from Settings.
- **Back up the phone** before you hand it over, so you don't lose anything in a swap.

## If your screen looks fine
Open and close it gently and check the inner screen under bright light once in a while.
If something changes, you'll have a clear record of when it started.

Hope your Duo stays crack-free!
