Live or run a business in one of the Texas counties hit by the July storms?
The IRS has pushed a lot of your tax deadlines to **Feb. 1, 2027**.

Here's who qualifies and the one big thing that's *not* covered.

![Texas IRS relief card](/assets/cards/us-irs-texas-july-storms-tax-relief-feb-2027-summary.png)

## Who qualifies
Individuals and households that live, or have a business, in **62 Texas counties** affected by severe storms that began on **July 14, 2026**.

The list includes some of the state's biggest counties:
**Harris, Fort Bend, Bexar, Travis, Williamson, Galveston, Brazoria, Hays, Comal and Webb**, among many others.

## Every county on the list
Atascosa, Austin, Bandera, Bastrop, Bexar, Blanco, Brazoria, Brewster, Burnet, Caldwell, Colorado, Comal, Crockett, DeWitt, Dimmit, Edwards, Fayette, Fort Bend, Frio, Galveston, Gillespie, Goliad, Gonzales, Grimes, Guadalupe, Harris, Hays, Jackson, Karnes, Kendall, Kerr, Kimble, Kinney, La Salle, Lavaca, Lee, Live Oak, Llano, Mason, Matagorda, Maverick, McMullen, Medina, Menard, Mitchell, Pecos, Real, Schleicher, Sutton, Terrell, Travis, Uvalde, Val Verde, Victoria, Waller, Washington, Webb, Wharton, Williamson, Wilson, Zapata and Zavala.

## What gets postponed
Filing and payment deadlines that fall **on or after July 14, 2026, and before Feb. 1, 2027**, now move to **Feb. 1, 2027**.

- **Extended 2025 returns:** if you had a valid extension, you now have until Feb. 1, 2027, to file.
- **Estimated tax payments** originally due on or after July 14, 2026, are postponed to Feb. 1, 2027.
- **Quarterly payroll and certain excise returns** normally due July 31 and Nov. 2, 2026.

## The catch: 2025 tax payments
Payments for 2025 returns were due **April 15, 2026**.
The IRS says those payments are **not eligible** for this relief, even if your filing deadline moved.

## Do you need to apply?
No, not if your address is in the disaster area.
The IRS **automatically** identifies taxpayers there and applies the relief.

If you got a late-filing or late-payment penalty notice for a date inside the relief window, call the number on the notice to have it removed.

If you live outside the area but your records are inside it, call IRS Special Services at **866-562-5227**.

Stay safe, Texas, and keep this date on your calendar: **Feb. 1, 2027**.
