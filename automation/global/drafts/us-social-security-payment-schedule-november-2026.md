One November payment date is different this year. If your birthday falls between the 1st and the 10th, your Social Security payment arrives on **Tuesday, Nov. 10**, a day earlier than usual, because Wednesday, Nov. 11 is Veterans Day.

![The date that moves](/assets/cards/us-social-security-payment-schedule-november-2026-summary.png)

## November 2026 at a glance

| Group | November 2026 |
|---|---|
| Started before May 1997, or get both SS and SSI | Tue, Nov. 3 |
| Birthdays on the 1st–10th | Tue, Nov. 10 |
| Birthdays on the 11th–20th | Wed, Nov. 18 |
| Birthdays on the 21st–31st | Wed, Nov. 25 |

![Who gets paid when in November 2026](/assets/cards/us-social-security-payment-schedule-november-2026-schedule.png)

The Wednesday pattern comes from the SSA's 2026 payment calendar. Second Wednesday for the 1st–10th, third for the 11th–20th, fourth for the 21st–31st. In November 2026 those Wednesdays fall on the 11th, 18th and 25th.

## Why Nov. 10 instead of Nov. 11

Veterans Day is a federal holiday, and it lands on the second Wednesday this year. When a payment date hits a weekend or a federal holiday, the SSA pays on the business day before. So the 1st–10th group gets paid Tuesday.

Nothing else moves. The Nov. 18 and Nov. 25 groups are paid on their normal Wednesdays.

## Where's my SSI?

If you get SSI, don't panic when nothing shows up on Nov. 1. Nov. 1, 2026 is a Sunday, so the November SSI payment already went out on Friday, Oct. 30. That's two SSI deposits in October and none in November. The next SSI payment is Tuesday, Dec. 1.

## No raise yet

November payments don't include the 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment for 2027. The higher amounts start with Social Security benefits payable in January 2027, and SSI recipients see their first increase on Dec. 31, 2026.

## A practical tip

If you pay a bill automatically around the 10th, the early deposit works in your favor this month. If your payment hasn't arrived a few business days after your date, check your my Social Security account or contact SSA before assuming it's lost.

Mark your date, and take care!
