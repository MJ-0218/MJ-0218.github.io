Open Enrollment for 2027 Marketplace health plans starts **Nov. 1, 2026**, and runs through **Jan. 15, 2027**. If you want your new plan to kick in on Jan. 1, the real deadline is **Dec. 15**. That's the date most people should circle.

![ACA Open Enrollment 2027 summary card](/assets/cards/us-aca-open-enrollment-2027-summary.png)

## The three dates that matter

Here's the whole calendar in one place:

![Key ACA dates](/assets/cards/us-aca-open-enrollment-2027-dates.png)

- **Nov. 1:** the first day you can enroll, renew, or switch plans through the Marketplace.
- **Dec. 15:** the last day to enroll or change plans and still have coverage start Jan. 1.
- **Jan. 15:** Open Enrollment ends. Sign up between Dec. 16 and Jan. 15 and your coverage starts Feb. 1 instead.

One detail people miss: coverage only starts once you pay your first premium. Picking a plan isn't the finish line.

## Already have a Marketplace plan?

Don't just let it roll over without a look. Plans and prices can change from one year to the next, and so can your own situation. Starting Nov. 1 you can update your application with the changes you expect for 2027, then compare what's available. It takes a little time, but it's the one chance most people get all year.

## Four ways to apply

HealthCare.gov lists four options:

1. **Online at HealthCare.gov.** It's the fastest. You'll log in or create an account.
2. **By phone.** Call 1-800-318-2596 for free help filling out the application and comparing choices.
3. **Through an approved enrollment partner,** like an insurance company or an online health insurance seller.
4. **On paper.** Mail in an application and you'll get eligibility results by mail within 2 weeks. That's slow, so don't leave it for December.

## Missed it? Two exceptions

After Jan. 15 you generally can't sign up until next fall, with two big exceptions:

- **Special Enrollment Period.** A life event, like moving, losing other coverage, getting married or having a baby, can open a window to enroll between Jan. 16 and Oct. 31.
- **Medicaid and CHIP.** You can apply for these free or low-cost programs at any time of year if you qualify.

Open Enrollment opens in 13 days. Set a reminder for Dec. 15, and good luck finding a plan that fits!
