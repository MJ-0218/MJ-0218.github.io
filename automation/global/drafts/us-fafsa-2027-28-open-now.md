Heading to college or trade school next fall? Your financial aid paperwork can start now.
The **2027–28 FAFSA** (Free Application for Federal Student Aid) is available, according to Federal Student Aid at the U.S. Department of Education.

Its announcement is dated **Sept. 23, 2026**, so families have had a few weeks' head start already.

![2027-28 FAFSA card](/assets/cards/us-fafsa-2027-28-open-now-summary.png)

## Which school year is this for?
This form covers college or career/trade school between **July 1, 2027, and June 30, 2028**.
So if you're a high school senior now, this is the FAFSA you need.

Current college students who want aid next year file this one, too.

## Did you already file during testing?
Some students filled out the form during the early testing period.
If yours went through successfully, FSA says you **don't need to resubmit** it.

## How long does processing take?
Most FAFSA forms are **processed immediately**.
Some can take **one to three days**.

Once yours is processed, you can review your **FAFSA Submission Summary**, and your information is sent to the schools you picked.

## A simple game plan
- **Set up your StudentAid.gov login early,** and ask parents or other contributors to do the same if they'll be on your form.
- **Gather tax info and records** before you start, so you can finish in one sitting.
- **List every school you're considering.** Adding schools now means they get your info right away.
- **Check each school's and your state's aid deadlines.** They're set by each school and state, so look them up instead of guessing.
- **Read your Submission Summary** carefully, and fix any mistakes quickly.

## Why file early?
Schools build aid offers from what your FAFSA says.
The sooner it reaches them, the sooner you can compare offers side by side.

Good luck with applications, and don't leave free money on the table!
