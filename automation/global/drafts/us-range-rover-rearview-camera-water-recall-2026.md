Driving a newer Range Rover? Your backup camera could be part of a recall.
Land Rover is recalling certain **2022–2026 Land Rover Range Rover** vehicles.

The fix is free, and it's a quick one at the dealer.

![Range Rover recall card](/assets/cards/us-range-rover-rearview-camera-water-recall-2026-summary.png)

## What's wrong
Water may not drain away from the **rearview camera** because there aren't enough drain holes.
Over time, that water can damage the camera.

If the camera image stops showing, you lose part of your view behind the SUV.
That raises the risk of a crash when you back up.

## The fix
Dealers will **drill extra drain holes** in the underside of the tailgate trim.
They'll also inspect the rearview camera and replace it if needed.

All repairs are **free of charge**.

## When you'll hear about it
Interim letters about the safety risk are expected to be mailed **Dec. 11, 2026**.
More letters will follow once the final remedy is ready.

## Who to call
- Land Rover customer service: 800-637-6837
- Land Rover's recall number: D171
- NHTSA campaign number: 26V678000

## Until then
Use your mirrors and turn to look before reversing, even if the camera seems fine.
If the screen goes blank or blurry, mention this recall when you book service.

Stay safe on the road!
