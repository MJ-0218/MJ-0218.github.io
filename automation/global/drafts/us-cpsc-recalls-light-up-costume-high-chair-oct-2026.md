Halloween's days away, and some of this week's recalls land right in the costume box.
The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) announced these on **October 22, 2026**.

Here's what to look for at home, and exactly what to do. It'll take two minutes.

![CPSC recalls card](/assets/cards/us-cpsc-recalls-light-up-costume-high-chair-oct-2026-summary.png)

## 1. CIYTI Light-Up Princess Costumes
A blue dress with a light-up **crown and wand**, plus a remote control.
The package says **"CIYTI"** on the front.

**The problem:** the crowns, scepters and remotes hold **button batteries that kids can get to easily**.
CPSC warns that swallowed button or coin batteries can cause internal chemical burns and death.

**What to do:** take the costume away from children now and stop using it.
Contact the seller at **Norakid2026@outlook.com** for a full refund.

About **94** were sold on Amazon from February 2025 through June 2025.

## 2. Bamamon LED Projecting Finger Light Toys
Boxes of **32 finger lights** in mixed colors that project space-themed patterns like astronauts and rockets.
Each light contains **three button cell batteries**.

**What to do:** stop using them and email **bamamonproductrecall@outlook.com** for a refund.
About **6,533** were sold on Amazon.com from March 2025 through August 2026.

If you've bought party favors or goodie-bag fillers online, it's worth checking those too.

## 3. Costway Convertible High Chairs
High chairs that **convert into a table and chair set**, in purple, red, blue or brown with white.

**The problem:** they don't have the **passive restraint system** the federal high chair standard requires.
A child can slip through the gap between the tray and seat and become entrapped or fall.

**What to do:** stop using it and contact Costway for a **free replacement**. You'll get a prepaid label to send the old one back.
Call **844-242-1885** (9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT, Monday to Friday) or email **recall@costway.com**.

About **3,332** were sold at **Walmart and Target** stores and Costway.com from May 2023 through July 2026.

## 4. VQQQ Natural Gas Patio Heaters
Models **VQQQ-A1 and VQQQ-001**, with a bronze pole and a silver mushroom-shaped top.

**The problem:** parts can malfunction and **release gas**, a fire and burn risk.
**What to do:** stop using it, shut the gas valve, disconnect the hose and email **vqqqrecall@163.com** for a refund.

## Button battery safety, in one line
Keep anything with button batteries out of reach, and if you think a child swallowed one, get emergency help right away.

CPSC says no injuries have been reported for these four products so far.
Still, don't wait.
