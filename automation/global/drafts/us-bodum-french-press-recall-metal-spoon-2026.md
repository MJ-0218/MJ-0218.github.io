If there's a Bodum French press in your kitchen, there's one habit to drop today: **stirring with a metal spoon**.

Bodum USA has recalled about **5.9 million** French press coffee makers with replaceable glass carafes because the glass can break during use and cause **serious burns**.
You don't have to throw yours away, though.

![Bodum recall card](/assets/cards/us-bodum-french-press-recall-metal-spoon-2026-summary.png)

## What's the problem?
The glass carafe can **unexpectedly break** if it's damaged or cracked.
That includes damage from **stirring coffee with a metal spoon**.

Bodum has received **70 reports** of carafes breaking during use, including **48 reports of burns**.

## Which models are included?
**8-cup and 12-cup** French presses with replaceable glass carafes in these lines:

- Bistro
- Brazil
- Caffettiera
- Chambord
- Crema
- Java
- Jesper
- Kenya

The Bodum name appears on the frame and inner lid of most models, and it's engraved with warnings on the glass.
More photos and model details are on the recall website.

## Where were they sold?
Target, Walmart, coffee and home goods stores, and online at Bodum.com, Amazon, Target.com, Walmart.com and others, **from 1974 through October 2026**, for about **$15 to $65**.

Yes, 1974. If you've had one for years, it likely counts.

## What to do
1. **Stop using metal spoons** or utensils to stir coffee in the glass carafe, starting now.
2. **Contact Bodum** to get a free **plastic stirring spoon** and a **warning label**.
3. Don't use a carafe that's **cracked or damaged**.
