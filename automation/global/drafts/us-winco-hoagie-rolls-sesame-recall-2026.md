Sesame allergy in your house? Take a quick look at your bread drawer.
WinCo Foods is recalling its **Hoagie Rolls** because the label doesn't list sesame flour.

For everyone else, the rolls are fine. For people allergic to sesame, they could cause a serious reaction.

![WinCo hoagie rolls recall card](/assets/cards/us-winco-hoagie-rolls-sesame-recall-2026-summary.png)

## Which rolls

- **WinCo Foods Brand Hoagie Rolls**
- **UPC 2-70552-00649-2**
- Net weight **16 oz (1 lb) 454 g**
- **Best By dates from 9/10/2026 to 11/3/2026**

The date on the bag is printed like this: "Best if used by SEP 9".

## Where they were sold

WinCo Foods stores in **California, Idaho, Montana, Nevada, Oregon, Utah and Washington**.

## What went wrong

The manufacturer, United States Bakery, found that the packaging didn't declare sesame flour in the ingredient list or allergen statement.
No illnesses have been reported.

## What to do

If you or someone at home is allergic or sensitive to sesame, don't eat these rolls.
Return them to your nearest WinCo Foods store for a refund.

Questions? Call the WinCo Foods Customer Service Desk at **1-800-824-1706**.

Stay safe, and check those bread bags!
