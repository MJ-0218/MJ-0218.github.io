Own a Lexus NX from the last few model years? Check this one.
Toyota is recalling certain **2022–2024 Lexus NX250, NX350, NX350 Hybrid and NX450h+** SUVs.

The issue is with the dashboard, and the fix is a free software update at the dealer.

![Lexus NX recall card](/assets/cards/us-lexus-nx-instrument-cluster-recall-2026-summary.png)

## What's wrong
The instrument panel's combination meter **may fail to display certain warnings or indicators**.
Because of that, these SUVs don't meet the federal standard for controls and displays (FMVSS No. 101).

## Why it matters
If the dash doesn't warn you about low **engine oil pressure**, high **engine coolant temperature** or an **electrical charge** problem, you might keep driving when you shouldn't.
NHTSA says that increases the risk of a crash.

## The fix
Dealers will **update the combination meter software, free of charge**.
Owner letters are expected to be mailed **Dec. 7, 2026**.

## Who to call
- Toyota customer service: 1-800-331-4331
- Toyota's recall number: 26LA10
- NHTSA campaign number: 26V687000

## In the meantime
Keep an ear out for unusual engine sounds, smells or overheating, and pull over safely if something feels off.
You can also enter your VIN on NHTSA's recall lookup to confirm whether your SUV is included.

Drive safe!
