Got a block of raw milk cheddar in the fridge? Take a second to check the brand.
CDC is investigating an **E. coli outbreak** linked to **Graziers raw milk cheeses** from Sierra Nevada Cheese Company.

Over half of the people who got sick are kids 5 or younger.
And because this cheese keeps for a long time, some of it may still be sitting in home fridges.

![Graziers raw milk cheese recall card](/assets/cards/us-graziers-raw-milk-cheese-ecoli-recall-2026-summary.png)

## Which cheeses are recalled
Sierra Nevada Cheese Company recalled **all** Graziers raw milk cheese.
The recalled grass-fed raw milk varieties are:

- Medium cheddar
- Sharp cheddar
- Jalapeño jack
- Monterey jack

Stores sold them in **8-oz or 16-oz** packages.
Restaurants and wholesalers got them in **5-lb loaves**.

## How many people are sick
As of Sept. 25, CDC counts **13 cases in 9 states**.
Eight people were hospitalized, and **3** developed a serious kidney problem called HUS. No deaths have been reported.

Illnesses started between July 7 and Aug. 26, 2026.
CDC says the real number of sick people is likely much higher, because many people get better without seeing a doctor and are never tested.

## What to do
- Don't eat any recalled cheese. Throw it away or return it.
- Check your freezer too, not just the fridge.
- Wash anything that touched the cheese, like cutting boards and counters, with hot soapy water or in the dishwasher.

## When to call a doctor
Call your healthcare provider right away if you have:

- Diarrhea and a fever higher than 102°F
- Diarrhea for more than 2 days that isn't getting better
- Bloody diarrhea
- So much vomiting you can't keep liquids down
- Signs of dehydration, like peeing very little, a dry mouth, or feeling dizzy when you stand up

## A tip for families
CDC suggests choosing pasteurized milk and dairy products.
That matters most for children under 5, who are more likely to get seriously sick from foodborne illness.

Stay safe, and give that cheese drawer a quick look tonight.
