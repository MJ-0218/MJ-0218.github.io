The 2026 general election is **Tuesday, Nov. 3**. That's the date on state election calendars from California to Delaware to Florida. Here's what to sort out before you head to the polls, and when you might not have to wait until then.

![Election Day 2026 summary](/assets/cards/us-election-day-2026-summary.png)

## What ID should you bring?

It depends on your state. Each state sets its own voter ID rules, and most require identification to vote in person.

If your state asks for a photo ID, the usual ones are a driver's license, a state ID or a passport. Some states accept other documents instead.

One thing you can leave at home: your voter registration card. You don't need to bring it, though you can use it as a form of ID.

![ID that can work](/assets/cards/us-election-day-2026-id.png)

## First-time voter? Read this

Federal law adds a rule for some first-time voters. You must show ID on Election Day if you didn't register in person **and** didn't show ID when you registered.

In that case, ID isn't limited to a photo card. A current utility bill, bank statement, government check or paycheck can also work, as can any government document with your name and address.

## What's a provisional ballot?

Sometimes there's a question about whether you're eligible. Then you may be asked to cast a provisional ballot, which is kept separate until officials check.

You might have to come back within a few days with an acceptable ID. If you don't, the ballot won't count.

This can happen even with the right ID. A common trigger is a mismatch: the name or address on your ID doesn't match your voter registration. Think of a recent move or a name change after marriage.

## Can you vote early?

Probably. Most states let you vote in person during an early voting period, and in most of them you don't need an excuse.

Dates vary a lot by state. In Delaware, for example, early voting starts Oct. 22. Florida voters can choose between vote-by-mail, early voting and voting at the polls on Election Day.

Some states run early voting through absentee ballots, so check under "absentee voting" if you can't find early voting info.

## Where do you vote on Nov. 3?

Election Day can be less flexible than early voting. In Delaware, for example, voters can use any early voting site in their county, but on Nov. 3 they must vote at their assigned polling place. Your state or local election office can tell you where yours is and its hours.

And no, you can't vote online. That's not allowed in federal elections in the U.S.

## The bottom line

Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026. Take five minutes this week to look up your state's ID rules and polling place. Fix any name or address mismatch now, and consider voting early if your state allows it.
