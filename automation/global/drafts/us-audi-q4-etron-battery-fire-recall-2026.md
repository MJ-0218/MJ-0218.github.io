Drive an Audi Q4 e-tron? Here's a recall you'll want to act on today.
Audi is recalling certain **2024–2025 Q4 e-tron and Q4 Sportback e-tron** electric SUVs.

The concern is serious: the high-voltage battery **may fail and cause a fire**.
There's no repair yet, so the most important step is a simple one at home.

![Audi Q4 e-tron recall card](/assets/cards/us-audi-q4-etron-battery-fire-recall-2026-summary.png)

## What's wrong
NHTSA says the high-voltage battery in these EVs may fail.
If it does, it can start a fire, and a vehicle fire increases the risk of injury.

## What to do right now
Audi advises owners to **charge to a maximum of 80%** until the repair is done.
Most EVs let you set a charge limit in the car's settings or the app, so it's worth doing that now rather than remembering every night.

## When will there be a fix?
Not yet. The remedy is **still under development**.
Audi expects to mail interim letters about the risk by **Dec. 18, 2026**, and will send more letters once the fix is ready.

## Is my car included?
VINs for this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on Oct. 23, 2026.
Enter your 17-digit VIN on NHTSA's recall lookup, or check the Audi app.

## Who to call
- Audi customer service: 1-800-253-2834
- Audi's recall number: 93SK
- NHTSA campaign number: 26V698000

## A few extra habits for now
- Keep the charge limit at 80% at home and on public fast chargers.
- If you notice smoke, a burning smell or warning messages, park outside away from buildings and call 911.
- Save the interim letter when it arrives; you'll need it when the repair is announced.

Stay safe, and set that charge limit tonight.
