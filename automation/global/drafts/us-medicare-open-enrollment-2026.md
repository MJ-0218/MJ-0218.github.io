Medicare Open Enrollment for 2026 runs from **Oct. 15 to Dec. 7**. Any change you make kicks in on Jan. 1, 2027, as long as your plan gets your request by Dec. 7. That leaves about 7 weeks from today, which sounds like plenty. It goes fast.

![Medicare Open Enrollment 2026 summary card](/assets/cards/us-medicare-open-enrollment-2026-summary.png)

## What you can change before Dec. 7

This is the one window each year when almost everyone with Medicare can rethink their coverage. During Open Enrollment you can:

- Join, drop, or switch Medicare Advantage Plans, with or without drug coverage
- Move from Original Medicare to a Medicare Advantage Plan, or back the other way
- Join, drop, or switch a Medicare drug plan (Part D) if you're in Original Medicare

If you're happy with your plan, you don't have to do anything. It usually just continues. But plans change their costs and coverage every year, so it's worth a quick look even if you plan to stay put.

## When does the new plan start?

Coverage from a change you make now starts on Jan. 1, 2027. The catch is timing: the plan has to receive your request by Dec. 7. If you mail a paper form, send it well ahead of the deadline so it arrives in time.

## How to compare and join a plan

Start with the official plan finder at Medicare.gov/plan-compare. You can look at plans in your area side by side, including what your prescriptions would cost under each one.

Once you've picked one, contact the plan to join. You can call them, enroll on their website, or ask for a paper form and mail it back. Prefer to talk it through? Call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227). TTY users can call 1-877-486-2048.

## Quick checks before you switch

Here's the short version of what to look at for any plan you're considering:

1. **Your doctors.** Are they in the plan's network? Call the office to confirm. On Oct. 16, 2026, CMS proposed a rule aimed at making Medicare Advantage provider directories more accurate, so a phone call is still the safest check.
2. **Your prescriptions.** Is each one covered, and at what cost? The plan finder can show this if you enter your drugs.
3. **Total yearly cost.** Look at premiums plus what you'd pay when you actually use care, not just the monthly premium.

None of this is one-size-fits-all. If your situation is complicated, free help is available through 1-800-MEDICARE.

## Missed the deadline?

If you're already in a Medicare Advantage Plan, there's a second chance. The Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period runs Jan. 1 to March 31. During that window you can switch to another Medicare Advantage Plan, or drop yours and go back to Original Medicare, and you can join a separate drug plan too. It doesn't help people in Original Medicare who want to switch drug plans, though, so Dec. 7 is the date to circle.

New to Medicare this year? Your own sign-up window is different. The Initial Enrollment Period lasts 7 months around your 65th birthday.

Mark Dec. 7 on the calendar, give your plan a once-over this week, and take care!
