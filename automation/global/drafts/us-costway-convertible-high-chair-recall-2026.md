Parents, please take a quick look at your baby's high chair today.
Costway is recalling **convertible high chairs** that were sold at **Walmart and Target** stores and on Costway.com.

The problem: there's no passive restraint, so a child can slip through the gap between the tray and seat.
That can lead to entrapment or a fall.

![Costway high chair recall card](/assets/cards/us-costway-convertible-high-chair-recall-2026-summary.png)

## Which high chairs
These are Costway high chairs that **convert into a table and chair set**.
They came in several color combos, including purple and white, red and white, blue and white, and brown and white.

There's no tracking label on the chairs, so go by the description and where you bought it.

## Where and when they were sold
- Walmart and Target stores nationwide
- Online at Costway.com
- From **May 2023 through July 2026**
- For between **$84 and $100**

About **3,332** chairs are included. No injuries have been reported.

## What to do
Stop using the high chair right away.
Contact Costway for a **free replacement high chair**.

You'll be asked for a receipt or a photo of the chair, and you'll get a prepaid label to send the old one back.
Costway ships the replacement once it receives the recalled chair.

Costway's recall line is 844-242-1885, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT, Monday through Friday, or email recall@costway.com.

## A separate warning: bblüv Grow high chairs
CPSC is also telling families to **stop using bblüv Grow high chairs** and throw them away.
The restraint systems weren't preassembled, which poses deadly fall and entrapment hazards, the agency says.

The company hasn't agreed to an acceptable fix, so there's no remedy on offer.
These chairs have a gray plastic bucket seat, a gray tray with a white bblüv logo, and four wooden legs.

Keep your little one safe, and pass this on to any parent friends.
