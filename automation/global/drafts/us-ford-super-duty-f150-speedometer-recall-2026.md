Drive a newer Ford truck? Check your VIN on this one.
Ford is recalling **102,159** trucks because the **speedometer and tachometer** may give wrong readings.

The free fix isn't ready yet, so here's what to know right now.

![Ford recall card](/assets/cards/us-ford-super-duty-f150-speedometer-recall-2026-summary.png)

## Which trucks are included
The recall covers certain:

- **2025-2026 Super Duty:** F-250, F-350, F-450, F-550, F-600 and F-750
- **2026 F-150**

Not every truck of these models is affected, so check your **VIN** to be sure.

## What's wrong
The analog cluster's **tachometer and speedometer may become stuck or move erratically**.
That means the gauges can show the wrong speed or engine RPM.

Because of this, the trucks don't meet federal safety standard **FMVSS No. 101, "Controls and Displays."**

## Why it matters
NHTSA says a speedometer that doesn't show accurate speed can **increase the risk of a crash**.
You could be going faster than you think without realizing it.

## The fix (not available yet)
Dealers will **replace the instrument cluster module for free**.

- **Interim letters** warning owners about the risk were expected to go out on **Oct. 26, 2026**.
- A **second letter** will come once the remedy is ready, which Ford expects in **December 2026**.

## What to do now
1. **Check your VIN** on NHTSA.gov. VINs became searchable there on **Oct. 7, 2026**.
2. Watch your mail for Ford's letters.
3. Until the fix is ready, keep an eye on your gauges and drive with extra care if they look stuck or jumpy.
4. Questions? Call **Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332**. Ford's recall number is **26C46**. NHTSA's campaign number is **26V653000**.

Drive safe out there!
