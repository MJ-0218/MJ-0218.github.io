If your birthday falls between the 11th and the 20th, your October Social Security payment lands on **Wednesday, Oct. 21**. Born on the 21st or later? You're paid a week after that, on Oct. 28.

![Next Social Security payment day](/assets/cards/us-social-security-payment-schedule-october-2026-summary.png)

## How Social Security picks your payment day

It comes down to your birthday. The SSA's 2026 payment calendar splits most people into three Wednesday groups:

| Birth date | Pattern | October 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| 1st–10th | Second Wednesday | Oct. 14 (already paid) |
| 11th–20th | Third Wednesday | Oct. 21 |
| 21st–31st | Fourth Wednesday | Oct. 28 |

![Who gets paid when in October 2026](/assets/cards/us-social-security-payment-schedule-october-2026-schedule.png)

So if you were born on the 5th, your October money already came on Oct. 14. Nothing else is coming this month.

## A quick way to find your own date

Not sure which group you're in? Look at the day of the month you were born, not the month. Then match it to the table above.

A few things worth checking while you're at it:

- **Direct deposit.** Most payments arrive by direct deposit. Your bank may post it early in the morning or later in the day, so a midday check is fair.
- **Your benefit letter.** Your award letter or your my Social Security account shows the amount you should expect, which helps you spot a mistake fast.
- **Bills tied to the 1st.** If rent or a car payment is due on the 1st and you're paid on a fourth Wednesday, a reminder a week early can save a late fee.

## Not on a Wednesday? You may be on the older schedule

Some people never switched to the Wednesday system. If you started getting Social Security before May 1997, you're generally paid on the 3rd of the month instead.

The same goes for people who get both Social Security and SSI. Their SSI usually comes on the 1st and their Social Security on the 3rd.

## Why November's SSI shows up in October

SSI is normally paid on the first of the month. Nov. 1, 2026 is a Sunday, though, so the November SSI payment goes out early, on Friday, Oct. 30.

That means you'll see two SSI deposits in October and none in November. It's not a bonus. Budget for it like your November check, because that's what it is. The next SSI payment after that is Tuesday, Dec. 1.

## When the 2027 raise shows up

The 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment announced this month isn't in your October or November money yet. It starts with Social Security benefits payable in January 2027. SSI recipients see it a little earlier, on Dec. 31, 2026.

## If your payment doesn't arrive

Give it a few business days, especially if you're paid by paper check. If it still hasn't shown up, check your my Social Security account or call SSA before you assume it's lost.

Mark Oct. 21 or Oct. 28 on your calendar, whichever is yours, and take care!
